What Goes Wrong With The Wrong Provider

Quote Surprises

Many providers offer vague or verbal quotes without a detailed, written scope, leading to unexpected costs when parts or labor fall outside initial estimates. In Dubai’s regulated environment, this can cause budget overruns and delays, especially when dealing with specialized systems like FAHU/FCU units or DEWA-compliant electrical panels. Without clear exclusions lists, clients often find themselves paying for ‘additional’ services not originally disclosed, eroding trust and complicating budgeting for property management or commercial operations.

Sub-contracted Quality

Using multiple subcontractors compromises accountability and service quality, especially in Dubai’s complex regulatory framework involving RERA, Civil Defence, and DHA standards. Subcontracted technicians may lack specific certifications or familiarity with Dubai’s climate challenges such as extreme heat and salt exposure, resulting in subpar repairs and increased equipment failure rates. This fragmented approach risks non-compliance with local codes and extends downtime, impacting both residential comfort and commercial productivity.

No Warranty Honoured

Contracts lacking warranty guarantees place clients at risk of having to pay repeatedly for recurring issues. Many providers do not stand behind their work or parts used, leaving property owners exposed to ongoing maintenance costs. In Dubai’s hot climate, where equipment like HVAC and electrical systems face accelerated wear, honoring warranties is critical to long-term operational efficiency and compliance with Dubai Municipality and Fire & Life Safety Codes.

Insurance / OA / Deposit Exposure

Without proper insurance and adherence to Owners Association (OA) regulations, clients can face liabilities for accidents or damages during maintenance. Additionally, providers who demand large upfront deposits without transparent policies expose clients to financial risk. Dubai’s regulatory environment requires licensed, insured contractors who respect OA guidelines and provide clear terms to protect residential and commercial property owners from unexpected liabilities and disputes.
