FAQ

What types of fire alarm systems are available in Dubai?

In Dubai, the most common types of fire alarm systems include conventional, addressable, and wireless systems. Conventional systems divide a building into zones, while addressable systems identify the exact detector triggering the alarm. Wireless systems offer flexible installation without extensive wiring. The choice depends on building size, complexity, and regulatory requirements.

Are fire alarm systems mandatory in Dubai residential buildings?

Yes, fire alarm systems are mandatory in most residential buildings, including villas and apartments, as per Dubai Civil Defence regulations. The requirements vary by building size and occupancy, but all must have functional smoke or heat detectors and audible alarms to protect occupants.

How often should fire alarm systems be serviced in Dubai?

The standard in Dubai is to service fire alarm systems at least once a year, though commercial buildings often require more frequent inspections. Routine maintenance ensures detectors, control panels, and alarm sounders function correctly and remain compliant with regulations.

What should I do if my fire alarm keeps sounding falsely?

Frequent false alarms usually indicate sensor contamination, low batteries, or electrical faults. The best fix is to call a professional to inspect and clean the detectors, check wiring, and replace faulty components. Ignoring false alarms can lead to system desensitization and increased risk.

Are smart fire alarm systems available in Dubai?
