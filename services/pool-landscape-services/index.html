The Definitive Guide to Pool & Landscape Maintenance in Dubai

What is pool and landscape maintenance in Dubai, and why does it require a specialist? Pool and landscape maintenance is the recurring care of swimming pools, irrigation systems, planted areas and hardscape elements of a Dubai property so that water stays balanced, equipment stays running, plants survive 45–50°C summers and the entire outdoor environment remains compliant with Dubai Municipality and DEWA regulations. In a typical European or Asian city this is a simple weekly chore. In Dubai, evaporation rates exceed 12 mm per day in summer, chlorine demand triples, top-up water arrives at 35°C with high TDS, sand storms coat every surface and salt-laden air corrodes metal fixtures within months. What Pool Maintenance in Dubai Actually Includes

A full residential pool service contract from Handyman.ae includes the following on every weekly or twice-weekly visit, with a written log emailed to the property owner:

  • Water testing — free chlorine, pH, total alkalinity, calcium hardness, cyanuric acid stabiliser, phosphates and TDS, measured with a Taylor K-2006 kit, not strip tests.
  • Chemical dosing — chlorine to 1–3 ppm, pH 7.2–7.6, alkalinity 80–120 ppm, cyanuric acid below 50 ppm — Dubai Municipality residential pool targets.
  • Surface skimming and floor vacuuming — manual or robotic (Maytronics Dolphin, Zodiac MX or Polaris) depending on pool size.
  • Brushing — waterline tile, walls, steps and benches to prevent scale and algae anchor points.
  • Skimmer basket and pump strainer cleaning — critical in Dubai because of palm fronds, bougainvillea petals and wind-blown sand.
  • Filter check and backwash — sand, cartridge or DE filter, with full media replacement every 5–7 years.
  • Equipment inspection — pump motor temperature, salt chlorinator cell condition, heater function, automatic top-up valve, lighting transformer.
  • Water level top-up — managed automatically where a fill valve exists; manually otherwise.

For pools that have gone green or have been neglected, our deep cleaning team performs a single-visit recovery using triple-shock chlorination, a phosphate remover and a 24-hour pump cycle before transitioning the pool onto a regular service contract.

Irrigation Systems: The Hidden 60% of Your Garden's Water Bill

Most Dubai villas waste between 40% and 60% of their irrigation water through unmaintained drip lines, broken emitters, blown poly-tube fittings, miscalibrated controllers and seasonal over-watering. Our irrigation audit corrects all of this. Typical scope:

  1. Pressure-test the mains feed and check the backflow preventer.
  2. Flush every zone and replace clogged or chewed emitters.
  3. Re-route exposed poly-tube to UV-protected positions and bury where possible.
  4. Re-programme the controller to seasonal schedules and install a rain or moisture sensor.
  5. Repair or replace solenoid valves (Hunter, Rain Bird, Toro).
  6. Document the zone map and emit-rate per zone in a one-page report.

For villas converting from sprinkler turf to drip-irrigated xeriscape, we also coordinate the trench cutting, polyethylene mains run and electrical for the controller transformer with our outdoor maintenance crew.

Compliance: What Dubai Regulators Demand

Dubai's regulatory framework around pools and landscape is more demanding than most owners realise:

  • Dubai Municipality Pool Safety Code — mandatory equipotential bonding, GFCI/RCD protection, free chlorine 1–3 ppm, weekly logbook for community pools.
  • DEWA outdoor electrical regulations — IP65 minimum fixtures, RCD on all outdoor circuits, transformer-fed low-voltage landscape lighting where practical.
  • Dubai Civil Defence pool fencing requirements — 1.2 m minimum self-closing barrier for villa pools where children may be present.
  • RERA tenancy law — pools and landscape in a rented villa are landlord-maintained unless explicitly contracted to the tenant; failure to maintain is grounds for rent dispute. See our annual maintenance contracts built for landlords.
  • Approved plant list — Municipality discourages high-water non-native species in new master communities; our designs adhere to the approved adapted list.

Warning Signs That Demand a Call-Out Today

Book a same-day visit if you observe any of the following — early intervention saves 5–10× the repair cost later:

  • Pool water turning green, cloudy or milky white within 48 hours of a service.
  • Strong chlorine smell — this is chloramine and means free chlorine is depleted.
  • Pump motor surging, screeching or tripping the breaker.
  • Salt chlorinator showing "low salt" alarm despite a recent salt addition.
  • Underwater light flickering or showing water ingress at the niche.
  • Drip-irrigation puddling or, conversely, plants wilting on a watered zone.
  • Yellowing or sudden defoliation of palms — suspect red palm weevil.
  • Coping or deck tiles lifting, cracking or showing efflorescence.
  • Outdoor lighting circuit tripping after rain or irrigation cycle.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should a pool in Dubai be serviced?

Residential pools in Dubai should be serviced at least once a week year-round. During the May–September summer, twice-weekly visits are recommended because evaporation, chlorine demand and algae pressure all spike with surface temperatures above 32°C. Community and commercial pools are required by Dubai Municipality to be tested and logged daily.

What chemicals are required for pool water balancing in Dubai?

Standard balancing uses chlorine (stabilised trichlor or liquid sodium hypochlorite), pH increaser/decreaser, alkalinity increaser, calcium hardness adjuster and cyanuric acid stabiliser. Dubai Municipality guidelines target free chlorine 1–3 ppm, pH 7.2–7.6, alkalinity 80–120 ppm and cyanuric acid below 50 ppm for residential pools.

Why is my pool turning green even with chlorine?

Green pool water in Dubai is almost always algae bloom caused by phosphate-rich top-up water, high cyanuric acid "locking" chlorine, or a failing filter. The fix is a triple shock with calcium hypochlorite, a phosphate remover, a full filter backwash and a 24-hour pump run. If algae returns within a week, the filter media or pump flow is the underlying cause.

Do I need a licensed contractor for pool equipment repair?

Yes. Any work on the pump, electrical bonding, heater or chlorinator must be performed by a DEWA-compliant electrician and a Dubai Municipality–registered pool contractor. Handyman.ae carries both licences in-house.

What plants survive a Dubai summer with minimal water?

Ghaf, Sidr, Damas, Bougainvillea, Desert Rose, Frangipani and Date Palm all survive 45°C summers on drip irrigation as low as 4–6 litres per plant per day. They are the backbone of every Handyman.ae sustainable landscape brief.

How much does pool and landscape maintenance cost monthly?

Pricing ranges from AED 600 for a small plunge pool with weekly service to AED 2,500–4,000 for a villa with pool plus 200–400 sqm of garden serviced twice weekly. Mansion-tier contracts can reach AED 7,500 a month for dedicated-tech, multi-discipline coverage.

Is landscape lighting regulated by DEWA?

Yes. All outdoor low-voltage and 230V landscape lighting must be installed by a DEWA-licensed electrician with IP65-rated fixtures, RCD protection and proper earthing. Pool-area lighting additionally requires equipotential bonding under DM Pool Safety Code section 7.
