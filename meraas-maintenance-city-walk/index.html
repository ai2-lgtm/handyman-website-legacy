The Complete Checklist (Step By Step)

Step 1

Inspect podium-deck waterproofing membranes for cracks, blisters, or delamination; verify drainage outlets are unobstructed and compliant with UAE Fire & Life Safety Code 2018; check sealant integrity around expansion joints; confirm membrane manufacturer warranties remain valid; document any signs of water ingress or ponding.

Step 2

Test Building Management System (BMS) integration with all MEP subsystems including HVAC, lighting, and fire alarms; verify communication protocols with Schneider Electric or Honeywell controllers; update BMS firmware to latest versions compliant with Dubai Civil Defence; validate alarm thresholds and emergency shutdown sequences; review historical data logs for anomalies.

Step 3

Service premium Fan Coil Units (FCUs) from Daikin and Carrier by cleaning filters, inspecting condensate drainage, testing motor amperage, and calibrating thermostats; replace worn gaskets and belts as per manufacturer guidelines; check refrigerant pressure and recharge if necessary; ensure compliance with HACCP standards for indoor air quality.

Step 4

Inspect Empower district cooling metering units for accuracy; verify valve actuators and pressure sensors; clean strainers and check for leaks in chilled water piping; coordinate with Empower for scheduled flow and temperature verification; confirm billing data matches consumption to avoid discrepancies.

Step 5

Review electrical panels with MCB/RCD units from Schneider or ABB; conduct insulation resistance testing; verify proper labeling and access; confirm compliance with Dubai Municipality electrical safety codes; test emergency power backup systems and UPS functionality.

Step 6

Assess Meraas Owners Association (OA) aesthetic standards on all visible MEP installations; check for corrosion, paint touch-up, and fixture alignment; verify signage and NOC compliance; ensure all external units are shielded from Shamal winds and salt exposure as per RDC guidelines.

Step 7

Compile all inspection and maintenance reports; schedule follow-up actions for urgent repairs; update maintenance logs in compliance with RERA and Ejari documentation; communicate findings with building management and OA committee; plan capital expenditure budgeting for next cycle.

How often should podium-deck waterproofing be inspected in City Walk Premium Towers?

Regular inspection ensures compliance with Dubai Civil Defence and RERA guidelines, preventing costly water ingress and structural damage.

What role does the Building Management System (BMS) play in Meraas maintenance at City Walk?

The BMS is critical for real-time monitoring and control of all MEP systems within City Walk Premium Towers. Proper maintenance includes firmware updates, communication protocol verification, alarm configuration, and data log analysis. This ensures compliance with Dubai Civil Defence and DEWA regulations, optimizes energy consumption, and enables proactive fault detection, reducing downtime and operational costs.

How is Empower district cooling managed and maintained in City Walk?

Empower district cooling in City Walk requires precise metering, regular valve actuator inspections, and chilled water pipe leak detection to maintain system efficiency and billing accuracy.
