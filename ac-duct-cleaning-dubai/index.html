Frequently Asked Questions

How often should AC ducts be cleaned in Dubai?

Most Dubai homes benefit from duct cleaning every 12 months because of dust and sand in the air. If anyone in the home has allergies, or the AC smells musty, we recommend every 6 months.

How long does AC duct cleaning take?

A typical apartment or villa takes 2-4 hours depending on the number of AC units and the length of ductwork. We'll confirm a time estimate before we start.

Are the AC coils cleaned as well as the ducts?

Yes. Our duct cleaning service includes the indoor coil, drain line flush and grilles, so the whole system is cleaned, not just the visible ducting.

Is AC duct cleaning worth it?

Clean ducts improve airflow and air quality and can lower your DEWA bill by letting the AC run more efficiently. It also removes dust, allergens and odours a filter alone won't catch.

Which areas of Dubai do you cover for duct cleaning?

We cover more than 65 Dubai communities with in-house technicians, with same-day appointments usually available.
