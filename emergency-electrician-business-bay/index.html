What To Do In The First 60 Seconds

In the critical first minute of an electrical emergency in Business Bay, your priority is safety and containment. Immediately isolate the affected electrical circuit by switching off the relevant MCB or RCD to prevent further damage or hazard. If unsure, switch off the main electrical panel only if safe to do so, avoiding any risk of electric shock. Simultaneously, evacuate the immediate area to protect occupants from potential fire or electrocution, especially in mixed-use buildings like The Opus or Capital Bay where balconies and IP44-rated fixtures can be vulnerable to faults.

Next, document the failure by taking clear photos or videos of the affected area and any visible damage. This evidence is essential for Owner Association (OA) coordination and DEWA reporting, ensuring compliance with Dubai Civil Defence and UAE Fire & Life Safety Code 2018 requirements. Avoid attempting repairs yourself to prevent voiding warranties or non-compliance with RERA and Ejari regulations. Once isolated and documented, contact a licensed emergency electrician immediately for professional intervention.
