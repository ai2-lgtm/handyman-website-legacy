Frequently Asked Questions

Why do Bluewaters Residences experience so much mould despite their premium construction?

Bluewaters Residences experience mould due to their coastal location exposing façades to salt-laden humidity and heavy monsoon-style rains that degrade curtain wall sealants. The mixed-use podium design combined with VRF/VRV HVAC systems limits uniform dehumidification, allowing moisture pockets to form behind wardrobes and in bathrooms. The salt accelerates corrosion of AC condenser coils, reducing HVAC efficiency and increasing indoor humidity, which promotes mould growth. Additionally, waterproofing membranes on terraces and podium decks can fail over time, causing water ingress and concealed mould. These factors cumulatively create an environment conducive to persistent mould despite premium finishes and materials.

How does the VRF/VRV system in Meraas townhouses affect mould growth?

VRF/VRV systems common in Meraas townhouses such as those in La Mer and Nikki Beach Residences provide localized cooling but lack the comprehensive dehumidification capabilities of district cooling systems like Emicool or Empower. This results in uneven humidity control within units, especially in enclosed spaces like wardrobes and bathrooms with concealed plumbing. Reduced moisture extraction allows relative humidity to remain elevated, fostering mould development on gypsum walls and wooden cabinetry. Additionally, compressor faults localized to the unit level can impair system performance, further increasing moisture retention. Proper maintenance and optimization of VRF/VRV systems are therefore critical to controlling mould in these developments.

What signs indicate mould behind built-in wardrobes in Meraas properties?

Visible black spots along wardrobe backs or bottoms, a persistent musty smell, and peeling paint or warped wood are clear indicators of mould behind built-in wardrobes in Meraas properties like Nad Al Sheba Gardens and J1 Beach. These wardrobes often lack adequate ventilation and are exposed to coastal humidity, creating ideal conditions for fungal growth. Moisture trapped from HVAC inefficiencies or envelope leaks accumulates unnoticed, allowing mould to thrive hidden from view. Early detection requires moisture mapping and sometimes removal of panels for inspection. Addressing the root cause involves repairing external water ingress and improving indoor humidity control.

How does podium deck waterproofing failure cause mould at City Walk?

City Walk’s podium decks support residential units above and are constructed with waterproof membranes designed to prevent water ingress. Over time, thermal cycling and wear degrade these membranes, allowing water to penetrate into ceiling cavities below. This trapped moisture saturates gypsum ceiling boards and adjacent walls, creating an environment conducive to mould growth. The problem is compounded during Dubai’s monsoon-style downpours when heavy rains increase water infiltration. Visible signs include ceiling stains, damp patches, and musty odours. Remediation requires membrane replacement and drying before mould removal to prevent recurrence.

What role do Geberit concealed cisterns play in mould issues in Meraas bathrooms?

Geberit concealed cisterns, widely used in Meraas bathrooms for their sleek design, can inadvertently contribute to mould if plumbing leaks or condensation occur within wall cavities. These cisterns are installed behind drywall or tile finishes, making leaks difficult to detect early. Moisture accumulation behind the wall promotes fungal growth on gypsum boards and adjacent surfaces. Additionally, coastal humidity exacerbates condensation risks around these installations. Effective mould removal includes plumbing inspection, leak repair, and wall cavity drying. Preventative maintenance ensures the integrity of concealed plumbing systems to avoid mould recurrence.

Yes, mould issues can impact DEWA and insurance claims in Meraas properties. DEWA requires proper documentation of any water-related damage before approving adjustments or disconnections. Insurance providers may deny claims if mould damage is linked to poor maintenance or delayed remediation. For rental units, Ejari registration mandates disclosure of mould problems, affecting tenant agreements and deposit refunds.
