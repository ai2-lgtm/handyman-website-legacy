Leak Detection & Repair

Hidden leaks are one of the most common and destructive plumbing problems in Dubai. A small pinhole leak in a pipe behind a wall or under a floor can lead to mold growth, structural damage, and astronomical DEWA bills. Our specialists use advanced non-invasive technology to pinpoint the exact location of leaks without tearing up your property. We utilize acoustic sensors that can hear the sound of water escaping from a pipe, even through thick concrete. This allows us to be incredibly precise, often identifying the leak within a few centimeters of its actual location. This precision is vital in Dubai's luxury properties, where minimizing damage to expensive finishes is a top priority.

Once detected, we provide immediate repairs using high-grade materials. Whether it's a leaking faucet, a running toilet, or a burst main line, we act fast to stop the water and prevent further damage. Pump Repair & Installation

Many properties in Dubai require booster pumps to maintain adequate water pressure, especially in larger villas. A failing pump can lead to frustratingly low pressure or a complete lack of water. We specialize in the repair and installation of various pump types, including centrifugal, submersible, and variable speed drive (VSD) pumps. VSD pumps are particularly popular in modern Dubai homes as they adjust their speed based on demand, providing constant pressure while significantly reducing energy consumption. Our team is expert at programming these sophisticated systems to ensure they provide the perfect balance of performance and efficiency for your specific household needs.

Our technicians can troubleshoot electrical issues, replace worn-out seals, and calibrate pressure switches to ensure your pump operates smoothly and quietly. A noisy pump is often a sign of impending failure, and addressing it early can save you from a total loss of water. We also check the expansion tanks associated with your pump system, as a failed bladder in the tank can cause the pump to "cycle" too frequently, leading to premature motor failure. If your current pump is beyond repair, we can supply and install high-quality replacements from leading manufacturers, ensuring consistent water pressure throughout your home at all times. We only use brands that have a proven track record in the harsh UAE climate.

In addition to booster pumps, we also service drainage and sewage pumps, which are often found in basement levels or properties with low-lying drainage. These pumps are critical for preventing flooding and must be maintained with absolute reliability. We offer scheduled maintenance for these systems, including cleaning the pump pits and testing the high-level alarms. Our goal is to ensure that all the pumps in your home are working in harmony to provide a seamless and worry-free water management system. Whether it's bringing water in or taking waste out, Handyman.ae has the technical expertise to keep your pumps running perfectly, day and night.
