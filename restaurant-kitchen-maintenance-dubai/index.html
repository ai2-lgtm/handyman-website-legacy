The Complete Checklist (Step By Step)

Step 1

Step 2

Perform preventive maintenance on grease traps every 30 days, including grease removal and sludge disposal following DM environmental guidelines; inspect trap seals and inlet/outlet pipes for leaks; maintain detailed cleaning logs for HACCP audits; use eco-friendly disposal methods to comply with Dubai Municipality waste regulations.

Step 3

Service walk-in chillers quarterly to check temperature stability within 1-4°C range; clean condenser coils using appropriate solvents to prevent salt corrosion; verify refrigerant levels and inspect compressor operation using ABB or Schneider control systems; calibrate temperature sensors per HACCP standards; log maintenance activities with technician certification.

Step 4

Descale dishwashers monthly to prevent hard-water scale buildup using manufacturer-recommended agents such as those approved by Hager or Honeywell; check spray nozzles and filters for clogging; validate temperature and chemical dosing controls; record maintenance to satisfy DM food safety and HACCP documentation requirements.

Step 5

Renew Dubai Municipality food license annually, preparing all required documentation including valid pest control certificates, maintenance logs for kitchen equipment, and updated HACCP plans; submit applications within stipulated timelines to avoid penalties; coordinate with DM inspectors for smooth approval.

Step 6

Maintain pest control contracts with licensed providers, ensuring monthly treatments and documentation of pest sightings; store records digitally for easy retrieval during DHA and DTCM audits; implement integrated pest management strategies to minimize chemical usage while maximizing effectiveness.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the role of HACCP in restaurant kitchen maintenance in Dubai?

HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points) is a systematic approach to food safety that identifies, evaluates, and controls hazards throughout the food preparation process. In Dubai, HACCP compliance is mandatory under Dubai Municipality regulations for restaurants. This checklist ensures critical control points such as temperature control in walk-in chillers, cleanliness of kitchen exhaust hoods, and proper pest management are maintained to prevent contamination and foodborne illnesses. Proper documentation as per HACCP standards also supports DM food license renewal and DHA inspections.

How often should the Ansul fire suppression system be inspected and serviced?

According to the UAE Fire & Life Safety Code 2018 and Civil Defence regulations, Ansul fire suppression systems integrated into kitchen exhaust hoods must be inspected quarterly by certified technicians. Inspections include verifying the integrity of nozzles, control panels, detection devices, and agent tanks. Immediate repairs or replacements are required if any deficiencies are found to ensure full operational readiness in case of a grease fire, protecting staff, property, and compliance status.

Why is grease trap maintenance important and how frequently should it be done?

Grease traps prevent fats, oils, and grease from entering Dubai’s sewage system, protecting public infrastructure and maintaining hygiene. Dubai Municipality mandates monthly preventive maintenance including grease removal, sludge disposal, and leak inspections. Failure to maintain grease traps can cause blockages, foul odors, and potential fines. Keeping detailed cleaning logs is essential for HACCP audits and DM compliance, ensuring uninterrupted restaurant operations.

What are the key pest control requirements for restaurants in Dubai?

Restaurants must engage licensed pest control providers to conduct monthly treatments targeting rodents, insects, and other pests. Pest control records must be meticulously maintained and presented during Dubai Health Authority (DHA) and Dubai Tourism and Commerce Marketing (DTCM) inspections. Integrated pest management practices minimizing chemical use are encouraged to comply with environmental standards while ensuring effective control, supporting DM food license renewals and customer health safety.

How does Dubai’s climate affect kitchen equipment maintenance?

Dubai’s extreme heat reaching 45°C in summer and frequent Shamal winds carrying salt humidity accelerate corrosion and wear on kitchen equipment such as walk-in chillers, HVAC units (FAHU/FCU), and plumbing fixtures. This climate necessitates rigorous preventive maintenance schedules including coil cleaning, corrosion-resistant materials, and regular calibration of temperature controls. Coordinating with district cooling providers like Empower or Emicool optimizes equipment efficiency and longevity, reducing energy consumption and operational costs.

What documentation is required for DM food license renewal related to kitchen maintenance?

For Dubai Municipality food license renewal, restaurants must submit updated HACCP plans, pest control certificates, and documented preventive maintenance logs for kitchen exhaust hoods, grease traps, walk-in chillers, and dishwashers. These records prove ongoing compliance with DM food safety regulations and support smooth renewal processes. Non-compliance or incomplete documentation may result in fines, license suspension, or closure, underscoring the importance of thorough maintenance and record-keeping.
