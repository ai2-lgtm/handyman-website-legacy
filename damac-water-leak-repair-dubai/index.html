Frequently Asked Questions

What causes water leaks in DAMAC Business Bay towers like Paramount?

Water leaks in DAMAC Business Bay towers such as Paramount primarily arise from condensate drainage failures in split DX air conditioning systems. The FCUs produce condensate collected in drip pans drained via pipes that can clog or corrode due to mineral deposits or poor maintenance. This causes overflow and water ingress into ceilings or walls. Additionally, decentralised condensate management limits system redundancy, increasing leak risk. The towers’ age, exposure to Dubai’s 45°C heat, and salt humidity accelerate pipe degradation. Diagnosing involves inspecting FCU trays, condensate lines, and BTU meters linked to Empower district cooling to confirm leak sources.

Why do AKOYA villas experience balcony waterproofing failures?

AKOYA villas, including those in DAMAC Hills 2 (Akoya Oxygen), experience balcony waterproofing failures due to rapid construction schedules and exposure to harsh environmental conditions. Waterproof membranes applied during build degrade under intense 45°C summer heat combined with salt-laden humidity, causing micro-cracks and delamination. These failures allow water to penetrate screeds and structural slabs, leading to damp patches and eventual structural damage. The villas’ fast-track construction often means waterproofing was not given optimal curing time, exacerbating failures. Repair requires membrane replacement and screed restoration aligned with Dubai Municipality standards.

How do pressurized water tank systems in DAMAC Hills contribute to leaks?

DAMAC Hills uses pressurized water tank systems with booster pumps to maintain adequate water pressure for villas. These pumps rely on mechanical seals which are prone to wear and failure over time, especially due to continuous operation in Dubai’s high temperatures. Seal failure causes water to leak inside pump enclosures or adjacent service areas, sometimes unnoticed until dampness or water stains appear. The pressurized nature amplifies leakage volume quickly, risking secondary damage. Repair involves seal replacement and pressure system testing to ensure reliable operation.

Can hidden water leaks in DAMAC properties damage the structure?

Yes, hidden water leaks in DAMAC properties can cause significant structural damage if left untreated. Water ingress behind gypsum boards weakens drywall and promotes mold growth. Persistent moisture accelerates corrosion of embedded MEP components like copper pipes and electrical conduits. Early detection and repair are critical to prevent escalating damage and costly restoration.

Our team manages these administrative protocols to expedite repair authorization.
