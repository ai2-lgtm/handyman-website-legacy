Our Specialized Handyman Solutions

Furniture Assembly & Installation

Struggling with flat-pack furniture? Our experts can quickly and securely assemble all types of furniture, from IKEA wardrobes to complex office desks. We ensure everything is perfectly aligned, sturdy, and ready for use, saving you time and frustration.

We also handle the safe dismantling of furniture for moving or storage, ensuring no parts are lost or damaged in the process.

TV Mounting & Audio-Visual Setup

Achieve the perfect viewing angle with our professional TV mounting services. We securely mount TVs of all sizes on any wall type, including drywall, concrete, and brick. We also conceal cables for a clean, modern look.

Beyond TVs, we can assist with the installation of soundbars, home theater systems, and projectors, creating the ultimate entertainment space in your home.

Carpentry & Woodwork

From repairing squeaky doors and broken hinges to custom shelving and cabinetry, our skilled carpenters deliver precision woodwork. We can restore damaged wooden furniture, install new skirting boards, and handle all your bespoke carpentry needs.

Our carpentry services also extend to outdoor areas, including decking repairs, pergola maintenance, and wooden fence restoration.

Drywall, Gypsum & Painting Prep

Unsightly holes, cracks, or water damage on your walls? We provide seamless drywall and gypsum repair services. We patch, sand, and prep surfaces perfectly, ensuring they are ready for a flawless coat of paint.

We also specialize in the installation of new gypsum partitions and false ceilings, allowing you to easily reconfigure your living or working space.

Tiling & Grouting Services

Whether it's a cracked floor tile in the kitchen or discolored grout in the bathroom, we can restore the beauty of your tiled surfaces. We offer tile replacement, regrouting, and sealing to prevent water damage and mold growth.

Our team is experienced in working with all types of tiles, including ceramic, porcelain, marble, and mosaic, ensuring a perfect finish every time.

Curtain & Blind Installation

Properly installed window treatments are essential for privacy and light control in Dubai. We expertly install curtain rods, tracks, roller blinds, Roman blinds, and Venetian blinds, ensuring they operate smoothly and look fantastic.

We can also assist with the installation of motorized blinds and smart curtain systems, integrating them seamlessly into your home automation setup.
