Circuit Breaker & Panel Upgrades

Your electrical panel, or distribution board, is the control center of your home's electricity. If you find that your breakers are frequently tripping, it's a clear sign that your system is overloaded or that there's a fault that needs immediate attention. Our experts specialize in upgrading old fuse boxes to modern circuit breaker panels that offer superior protection against short circuits and ground faults. We also install Residual Current Devices (RCDs) and Earth Leakage Circuit Breakers (ELCBs), which are essential for preventing electric shocks and are a mandatory requirement in Dubai.

Upgrading your panel not only improves safety but also allows for the expansion of your electrical system. If you're planning to install a new kitchen, a home theater, or an electric vehicle (EV) charging station, a panel upgrade is often necessary. Our team will calculate your total load requirements and install a panel that can handle your current and future needs, ensuring a stable and reliable power supply throughout your home or office.

Why does my circuit breaker keep tripping?

A circuit breaker that frequently trips is a safety mechanism designed to prevent electrical fires and damage to your appliances. The most common cause is an overloaded circuit, where too many high-power devices are running on the same line. In Dubai, this often happens when multiple AC units, water heaters, and kitchen appliances are used simultaneously. Other potential causes include short circuits, ground faults, or a faulty breaker that needs replacement.

If your breaker trips, try unplugging some devices and resetting it. If it trips again immediately or frequently, it's a sign of a deeper issue that requires professional attention. Our electricians use specialized diagnostic tools to trace the fault and determine the exact cause. We can then provide a permanent solution, such as redistributing the load across different circuits, repairing faulty wiring, or upgrading your electrical panel to handle your property's power demands more effectively.

Can you help reduce my DEWA electricity bills?

Yes, we can certainly help you improve the energy efficiency of your home, which can lead to significant savings on your DEWA bills. One of the most effective ways is by upgrading your lighting to modern LED fixtures. LEDs use up to 80% less energy than traditional bulbs and last much longer. We also offer the installation of smart thermostats and timers for water heaters and AC units, allowing you to optimize their usage based on your daily schedule.

Additionally, our electricians can conduct an energy audit of your property to identify "vampire" loads and inefficient appliances. We can also check your wiring for any leaks or faults that might be causing unnecessary power consumption. By ensuring your electrical system is functioning at peak efficiency, we help you reduce your environmental impact and keep more money in your pocket. Small changes can make a big difference in a city like Dubai where energy usage is naturally high due to the climate.
