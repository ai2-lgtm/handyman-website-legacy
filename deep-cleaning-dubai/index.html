Our Deep Cleaning Expertise

Whole-Home Deep Cleaning

Our whole-home deep cleaning service is a top-to-bottom, meticulous process that leaves no corner untouched. We systematically clean every room, focusing on removing accumulated dust, dirt, and allergens from ceilings, walls, baseboards, and floors. This comprehensive approach ensures a uniformly pristine environment throughout your entire residence, revitalizing your living space and creating a healthier atmosphere for your family.

We utilize specialized equipment to reach high and difficult-to-access areas, ensuring that even the most hidden dust bunnies are eradicated. Our team pays special attention to light fixtures, ceiling fans, and intricate architectural details, restoring their original luster. The result is a home that feels remarkably fresh, bright, and deeply sanitized.

Kitchen Deep Cleaning & Sanitization

The kitchen is the heart of the home but also a prime area for grease buildup and bacterial growth. Our kitchen deep cleaning service targets tough grease, baked-on food, and hidden grime. We meticulously clean and sanitize all countertops, backsplashes, sinks, and cabinetry, both inside and out. We also focus on degreasing exhaust hoods and cleaning the exteriors of all major appliances.

Special attention is given to areas that are notoriously difficult to clean, such as the spaces between appliances and countertops, and the intricate details of your stovetop. By employing powerful, yet safe, degreasing agents and sanitizing solutions, we ensure your kitchen is not only sparkling clean but also a hygienic environment for food preparation.

Bathroom Deep Cleaning & Descaling

Bathrooms require rigorous cleaning to combat soap scum, hard water stains, mold, and mildew. Our bathroom deep cleaning service involves intensive scrubbing and descaling of all surfaces, including tiles, grout lines, bathtubs, showers, sinks, and toilets. We use specialized products to dissolve stubborn mineral deposits and eliminate bacteria, ensuring a pristine and sanitary space.

We also focus on polishing fixtures, cleaning mirrors to a streak-free shine, and thoroughly sanitizing all high-touch areas. Our meticulous approach not only restores the aesthetic appeal of your bathroom but also eliminates unpleasant odors and prevents the growth of harmful microorganisms, providing you with a spa-like level of cleanliness.

Our specialized carpet and upholstery deep extraction service utilizes powerful hot water extraction techniques to penetrate deep into the fibers, lifting dirt, stains, and allergens that ordinary cleaning methods leave behind. This process not only revitalizes the appearance of your soft furnishings but also significantly improves indoor air quality.

We carefully assess the fabric type and condition before selecting the appropriate cleaning solutions and extraction methods. Whether you have delicate silk rugs or durable synthetic carpets, our expert technicians ensure a thorough and safe cleaning process. We also apply specialized treatments to tackle stubborn stains, such as pet accidents or wine spills, restoring your carpets and upholstery to their original beauty and freshness.

Furthermore, our deep extraction process helps to extend the lifespan of your carpets and upholstery. By removing abrasive dirt particles that can wear down fibers over time, we protect your investment and keep your furnishings looking newer for longer. We also offer optional fabric protection treatments that create an invisible barrier against future spills and stains, making routine maintenance easier and more effective.

We understand that drying time is a concern for many homeowners. We also utilize high-velocity air movers to expedite the drying process, minimizing disruption to your daily routine and preventing the growth of mold or mildew.

Window & Glass Deep Cleaning

Clean windows are essential for maximizing natural light and enjoying the stunning views that Dubai has to offer. However, the region's frequent dust storms and high humidity can quickly leave windows streaked and grimy. Our comprehensive window and glass deep cleaning service ensures that every pane of glass in your home is crystal clear and streak-free. We clean both the interior and exterior surfaces, as well as the frames, tracks, and sills, removing accumulated dirt, dust, and hard water stains.

For hard-to-reach windows or exterior glass on higher floors, our team is equipped with the necessary safety gear and extension poles to ensure a thorough and safe cleaning process. We pay meticulous attention to detail, ensuring that even the corners and edges are perfectly clean.

In addition to standard windows, our service also covers glass doors, skylights, mirrors, and glass balustrades. We understand that different types of glass require specific cleaning techniques, and our experienced technicians are trained to handle all varieties safely and effectively. We also offer specialized treatments to remove stubborn hard water stains or mineral deposits that can accumulate on shower glass or exterior windows exposed to irrigation systems.

Regular professional window cleaning not only enhances the aesthetic appeal of your home but also protects the glass from long-term damage. Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a deep cleaning service?

Our deep cleaning service is highly comprehensive. It includes intensive cleaning of all rooms, focusing on areas often missed during regular cleaning. This involves scrubbing and sanitizing bathrooms (including descaling), degreasing kitchens (including inside appliances if requested), dusting high and hard-to-reach areas, cleaning baseboards, doors, and frames, and thorough vacuuming and mopping of all floors. We tailor the service to address the specific needs of your property.

How long does a deep cleaning take?

The duration of a deep cleaning session depends entirely on the size and current condition of your property. A standard 1-bedroom apartment might take 4-6 hours, while a large villa could require a full day or a larger team. During our initial consultation, we will provide you with an estimated timeframe based on your specific requirements to ensure minimal disruption to your schedule.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies or equipment?

No, you do not need to provide anything. Handyman.ae brings all the necessary professional-grade cleaning supplies, eco-friendly solutions, and advanced equipment required to perform a thorough deep clean. Our teams are fully equipped to handle any cleaning challenge, ensuring a hassle-free experience for you.

Are your cleaning products safe for pets and children?

Yes, absolutely. The health and safety of your family and pets are our top priorities. We utilize high-quality, eco-friendly, and non-toxic cleaning products that are highly effective at removing dirt and bacteria but are completely safe for use around children and animals. We ensure no harmful chemical residues are left behind.

How often should I schedule a deep cleaning for my home?

For most homes in Dubai, we recommend scheduling a professional deep cleaning every 3 to 6 months. However, this can vary based on factors such as the presence of pets, allergies, the size of your family, and your lifestyle. Regular deep cleaning helps maintain optimal indoor air quality and preserves the condition of your home's interior.
