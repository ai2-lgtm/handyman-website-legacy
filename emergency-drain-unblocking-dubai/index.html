The Technical Mechanism Of The Failure

Drain blockages commonly originate from kitchen FOG buildup, where fats, oils, and grease solidify inside pipes, narrowing flow paths. In Dubai’s hot climate and high humidity, grease congeals faster, exacerbated by salt-laden air that accelerates corrosion of pipe walls. Soil-pipe ventilation systems, designed to equalize pressure and eliminate odors, fail when P-traps dry out due to evaporation in summer, allowing sewer gases to escape into living spaces.

Hair and debris accumulation in floor drains further restrict flow, creating localized backups. Blockages increase hydraulic pressure in soil stacks, risking joint failure or pipe bursts. Emergency jetting uses high-pressure water streams to dislodge deposits, while CCTV cameras verify pipe integrity and locate hidden damages.
