Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I find mould behind wardrobes in my DAMAC Hills villa?

Mould behind wardrobes in DAMAC Hills villas is typically caused by moisture ingress from balcony waterproofing failures and booster pump seal leaks in the pressurised water tank system. These issues allow water to accumulate inside wall cavities adjacent to wardrobes, creating a damp environment conducive to mould growth. The rapid construction of AKOYA villas within DAMAC Hills often resulted in less robust waterproofing membranes. Additionally, Dubai’s high summer temperatures and humidity exacerbate moisture retention in these areas. A master technician will inspect the balcony screed integrity and booster pump seals to identify and rectify the moisture source before treating the mould.

How does the HVAC system in Business Bay DAMAC towers contribute to mould problems?

Business Bay DAMAC towers like Paramount use split DX air conditioning systems instead of district cooling, decentralizing condensate drainage responsibility to individual FCUs. FCU condensate trays and drainage pipes can become blocked or leak, allowing water to seep into walls and closets, fostering mould growth. The absence of centralised condensate management increases the risk of unnoticed leaks. Furthermore, unbalanced electrical phase loading can cause MCB tripping, interrupting HVAC function and reducing ventilation effectiveness. Addressing these HVAC faults is essential to eliminate mould sources in Business Bay towers.

What is the role of booster pumps in mould formation in DAMAC Hills?

Booster pumps in DAMAC Hills pressurised water tank systems maintain water supply pressure but rely on seals that degrade over time. Seal failure leads to water leaks within wall cavities and ceiling voids, increasing humidity and dampness which promotes mould growth. This mechanism is unique to DAMAC Hills compared to other DAMAC communities that use direct mains water. Regular maintenance and timely seal replacement are critical to prevent moisture-related damage. Technicians must inspect booster pump assemblies during mould remediation to ensure the root cause is addressed.

Can district cooling systems in DAMAC properties prevent mould?

District cooling systems supplied by Empower or Emicool in certain DAMAC communities provide centralized chilled water for HVAC, reducing condensate formation compared to split DX systems. This decreases the likelihood of condensate leaks and related mould problems. However, many DAMAC towers in Business Bay and JLT do not use district cooling, relying instead on decentralized FCUs. Even in district-cooled buildings, poor waterproofing or plumbing leaks can still cause moisture accumulation. Therefore, while district cooling mitigates some mould risks, comprehensive building fabric integrity and maintenance remain essential.

How does the hot and humid Dubai climate affect mould in DAMAC buildings?

Dubai’s climate, with summer temperatures reaching 45°C and high salt humidity, intensifies mould growth in DAMAC properties by maintaining warm, moist conditions ideal for fungal proliferation. Salt-laden air accelerates corrosion of MEP components like FCU condensate trays and booster pump seals, increasing leak risk. West-facing elevations in AKOYA villas experience paint blistering due to solar exposure combined with moisture ingress. The climate exacerbates any existing waterproofing failures or condensate drainage issues, making regular inspection and rapid mould remediation indispensable in DAMAC communities.

What documentation is required for mould removal in DAMAC properties?

For mould removal in DAMAC properties, residents must obtain OA approval and submit NOC applications detailing the scope of works. Professional inspection reports and remediation certificates are necessary to comply with Ejari and RERA regulations, which mandate healthy living conditions for tenancy registration. Documentation must confirm that condensate leaks, waterproofing failures, and HVAC faults have been rectified.
