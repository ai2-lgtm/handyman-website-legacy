The Hidden Damage

Behind the scenes, untreated problems can cause structural damage, such as corrosion from Dubai’s salt-laden humidity weakening metal fixtures or water leaks damaging concealed cisterns common in Emaar towers. Additionally, non-compliance with Dubai Municipality and DEWA regulations risks penalties and invalidates insurance claims. The Social & Financial Cost

Failure to address handyman issues impacts neighbours through noise, water seepage, or shared system failures in multi-unit buildings. These social and financial burdens compound, making prompt, expert handyman service essential.
