The Technical Mechanism Of The Failure

Lock failures in Dubai Marina often arise from mechanical wear, electronic malfunction, or external environmental stressors. Mortice locks, prevalent in buildings like Princess Tower and Sulafa, suffer from internal spring fatigue and latch misalignment aggravated by frequent use and exposure to Dubai’s 45°C summer temperatures. Smart locks such as Yale and August models rely on integrated electronics powered by batteries or building power supplies; power surges or moisture ingress can cause circuit failures.

In addition, salt-laden coastal air accelerates corrosion of metal lock components and electrical contacts, while Shamal winds contribute to dust accumulation inside lock mechanisms. In multi-tower complexes, improper OA NOC coordination during cylinder changes or lock resets can cause synchronization errors within the overall access control system, requiring expert intervention to restore functionality without breaching DEWA or Civil Defence safety protocols.
