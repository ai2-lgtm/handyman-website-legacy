Frequently Asked Questions

Why does mould commonly appear behind wardrobes in Dubai Properties apartments?

Mould behind wardrobes in Dubai Properties units primarily results from concealed moisture accumulation caused by HVAC system failures and building envelope defects. Ageing FAHU units in JBR towers often have blocked condensate drains, causing water to leak into ceiling and wall voids. Additionally, chilled-water valve leaks behind gypsum partitions in Mudon and Villanova townhouses create damp microclimates. The lack of airflow behind wardrobes traps humidity, providing ideal conditions for black mould growth.

How does Dubai’s coastal climate affect mould growth in Dubai Properties communities?

Dubai’s coastal climate, with high salt humidity and 45°C summer temperatures, accelerates corrosion of external hardware and building envelope degradation in Dubai Properties communities. Salt-laden Shamal winds erode door cylinders and façade seals, allowing water ingress. Combined with condensation from chilled-water systems and inadequate ventilation, this promotes persistent mould growth, especially in JBR’s beachfront towers directly exposed to marine conditions.

What role do FAHU units play in mould development in JBR towers?

FAHU (Fresh Air Handling Units) in JBR towers condition humid coastal air but can contribute to mould if their condensate drain pans become blocked. This leads to water overflow into ceiling voids and gypsum partitions, creating damp environments ideal for mould spores. Regular maintenance is critical to keep drains clear and prevent ceiling stains and mould proliferation.

How can I prevent mould recurrence after removal in Mudon or Villanova townhouses?

Preventing mould recurrence involves maintaining Empower district cooling FCUs and ensuring chilled-water pipes are properly insulated to avoid condensation. Regular inspection of irrigation manifolds to detect back-siphon leaks and prompt repair prevents soil moisture infiltration into gypsum partitions. Additionally, applying anti-fungal paints and sealing building envelope joints reduces moisture ingress. Good ventilation practices inside the home also help regulate humidity.

What are the signs of mould-related electrical faults in Remraam apartments?

In Remraam, mould-related moisture intrusion can cause chilled-water riser leaks that affect electrical panels, leading to MCB (Miniature Circuit Breaker) tripping and RCD (Residual Current Device) faults. Residents may notice frequent power interruptions or appliance failures coinciding with wet weather or irrigation leaks. Prompt diagnosis and repair of water ingress are critical to prevent electrical hazards.

Does mould affect Ejari and insurance claims in Dubai Properties developments?

Yes, persistent mould and associated water damage can complicate Ejari tenancy renewals as landlords and tenants must demonstrate property condition compliance. Owner Associations may withhold approvals or impose penalties if mould results from neglect. Insurance claims for water damage require documented maintenance; failure to address mould promptly can lead to claim denials, increasing financial risk for property owners.
