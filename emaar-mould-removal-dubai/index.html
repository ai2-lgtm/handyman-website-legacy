Frequently Asked Questions

What causes mould behind wardrobes in Emaar Downtown Dubai apartments?

In Emaar Downtown Dubai apartments such as Burj Khalifa and Burj Vista, mould behind wardrobes is caused primarily by AC condensate leaks from FCU drain pans concealed above gypsum ceilings. These leaks saturate gypsum partition walls, creating damp conditions ideal for black mould growth. The lack of ventilation behind wardrobes compounds the problem. The typical mechanism involves FCU drain pan corrosion or blockage, leading to overflow. Since gypsum partitions absorb moisture, mould spores proliferate rapidly in these hidden cavities, requiring professional moisture detection and remediation.

How does district cooling contribute to mould in Emaar Beachfront properties?

District cooling in Emaar Beachfront is provided via Empower or Emicool systems which use BTU meters with valves to regulate chilled water flow. Valve failures or leaks in these BTU meters cause localized water seepage behind walls or under floors. This moisture accumulates in gypsum partitions and wooden finishes, promoting mould growth. The developer’s consistent use of concealed gypsum walls and centralized cooling infrastructure means this failure mode is common. Repair involves servicing BTU meter valves and drying affected areas thoroughly.

Why is mould removal more urgent in Emirates Living villas like The Springs?

In Emirates Living villas such as The Springs and The Meadows, mould removal is urgent due to ageing copper pipework and original FAHU units that frequently develop pinhole leaks. These leaks introduce moisture into wall cavities behind bathrooms and wardrobes, damaging gypsum partitions and woodwork. The older construction and limited ventilation exacerbate moisture retention. Delaying remediation risks structural damage and health hazards. Immediate leak repair and mould eradication are essential to preserve the villa’s integrity.

Can mould behind bathroom walls in Dubai Marina’s Marina Gate be linked to FCU drain pan issues?

Yes, in Dubai Marina’s Marina Gate, mould behind bathroom walls is often linked to corroded FCU drain pans leaking condensate into gypsum ceilings and partitions. The FCU units in these towers are original and susceptible to corrosion after years of service. The resulting dampness fosters mould growth in concealed spaces. Effective treatment requires inspection, FCU maintenance or replacement, and mould remediation to prevent recurrence.

How do gypsum partition walls affect mould growth in Emaar villas?

Gypsum partition walls used extensively in Emaar villas across Arabian Ranches and Emirates Living are porous and absorb moisture readily. When AC condensate leaks, copper pipe seepage, or BTU meter valve failures introduce water, gypsum retains this moisture, creating an environment conducive to mould proliferation. The concealed nature of these walls delays detection. Proper mould removal must include moisture source repairs and replacement or treatment of gypsum surfaces to restore dryness and prevent regrowth.

What role does ventilation play in mould prevention in Emaar communities?

Ventilation is critical in preventing mould in Emaar communities, especially in enclosed areas like wardrobes and bathrooms in Dubai Hills Estate and The Greens. Poor airflow traps moisture, allowing mould spores to settle and grow on damp gypsum and wood surfaces. Emaar’s building designs often limit natural ventilation in these spaces. Installing mechanical exhaust fans or improving air circulation disrupts mould growth conditions.
