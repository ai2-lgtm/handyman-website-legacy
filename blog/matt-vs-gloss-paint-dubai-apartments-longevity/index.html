Matt vs Gloss Paint for Dubai Apartments: Which Lasts Longer?

Matt vs Gloss Paint for Dubai Apartments: A Transformation Story

Dubai apartment owners often face the dilemma: should I pick matt or gloss paint? This decision affects the appearance, maintenance, and lifespan of walls, especially in high-rise buildings like JBR or Business Bay. Here we share a real transformation story from a Dubai Marina apartment where a switch from gloss to matt paint dramatically improved wall longevity and aesthetics in Dubai’s challenging environment.

Before: The Gloss Paint Dilemma

The apartment was freshly painted in gloss finish to achieve a shiny, modern look popular in the market. Initially, the gloss reflected light beautifully, enhancing the room’s brightness. However, within a year, problems arose:

  • Visible Imperfections: Gloss paint highlighted wall cracks and uneven surfaces caused by humidity and sandstorm dust.
  • Frequent Cleaning: Gloss surfaces required regular wiping to remove fingerprints and marks, common in busy families.
  • Peeling and Flaking: In the humid bathroom and kitchen areas, gloss paint started to peel near windows and plumbing fixtures.

After: The Matt Paint Revival

The decision was made to repaint using high-quality matt paint formulated for Dubai’s climate, focusing on the living room, bedrooms, and bathrooms. Results were remarkable:

  • Concealed Imperfections: The matt finish absorbed light, hiding minor wall cracks and surface irregularities common in older Dubai buildings.
  • Lower Maintenance: Matt surfaces did not show smudges as clearly and required less frequent cleaning.
  • Improved Durability: The specialized matt paint resisted humidity better, reducing peeling risks.

What Dubai-Specific Factors Affect Paint Choice?

  • Humidity and Sandstorms: Frequent in coastal areas like JBR, these accelerate paint degradation.
  • UV Exposure: High-rise windows face direct sunlight, affecting gloss paint’s sheen and color retention.
  • Building Settling: Older buildings in Downtown Dubai often develop minor cracks visible on glossy surfaces.

FAQs

1. Does matt paint last longer than gloss in Dubai?

Generally, matt paint is more forgiving to humidity and surface imperfections, making it last longer in apartments with less frequent repainting.

2. Is gloss paint suitable for kitchens and bathrooms?

Gloss paint is often preferred for these areas due to its water resistance but requires careful application and ventilation to prevent peeling.

3. Can I repaint over gloss with matt paint?

Yes, but surface preparation like sanding and priming is essential for proper adhesion.

4. How frequently should Dubai apartments repaint walls?

Typically every 3-5 years, but high-traffic or coastal apartments may require more frequent touch-ups.

5. What is the benefit of hiring licensed painting contractors?

Licensed contractors ensure paint compatibility, proper application, and compliance with building regulations, avoiding issues with Ejari and DEWA.
