What Is Actually Happening

Your Al Furjan townhouse is not new anymore. The fixtures installed during construction? They are past their prime. This isn't about cosmetic preference. This is about engineering. The internal components. The seals. The aerators. They degrade. Dubai's water quality, while treated, still contains minerals. Hard water. These minerals build up. Inside your taps. Inside your showerheads. Inside your toilet mechanisms. They cause microscopic abrasions. They corrode metal parts. They stiffen rubber washers. Then you get drips. You get leaks. You get reduced flow. The finish on the outside? That's just the visual symptom. Underneath, the unseen damage accumulates. Older fixtures were not designed for modern water conservation standards. They simply dump water. A 15-year-old showerhead can use 2.5 gallons per minute (GPM). A modern, efficient one? 1.5 GPM, sometimes even less. That's 40% less water, for the same shower. Your toilet from 2008 in Jumeirah Park? Probably a 6-liter flush. A new dual-flush model? 3 liters for liquid waste, 4.5 liters for solid. That's a 25-50% reduction per flush. These numbers add up. Daily. Monthly. Annually. The design itself is also a factor. Old fixtures are harder to clean. They have more crevices. They attract scale and mildew. Especially in Dubai's humidity. They harbor bacteria. The constant temperature fluctuations from the water heater. The high ambient temperatures. These factors accelerate material fatigue. Plastic components become brittle. Chrome plating peels. The result is a fixture that not only looks tired but performs poorly. It leaks more. It wastes more. It breaks more often. Warning Signs

Don't wait for disaster. Your fixtures are already telling you. If you see **visible rust or corrosion** on faucet bases or showerheads, it means the protective plating is gone. Water is attacking the base metal. Structural integrity is compromised. If your **water pressure is inconsistent or weak** in one specific faucet or shower, it means internal blockages. Mineral buildup. Calcification. The flow path is constricted. If you hear a **faucet dripping after you've turned it off**, it means worn-out washers or seals. The internal cartridge is failing. This is pure water waste. If your **toilet constantly runs or flushes weakly**, it means the flapper, fill valve, or flush mechanism is faulty. It's an older, inefficient design. It's draining your water tank unnecessarily. If the **handles are stiff, loose, or difficult to turn**, it means internal wear. The stem, O-rings, or cartridges are failing. A full break could mean an uncontrolled water gush. If the **finish is peeling, bubbling, or discolored**, it means the protective coating has failed. The aesthetic appeal is gone. It makes the whole bathroom look neglected. If you notice **slow drainage from a sink, but only when water is running through that specific tap**, it means internal pipe corrosion or significant buildup directly below the fixture. This is not a main drain issue; it's a fixture-related pipe problem. If you consistently have to **wiggle a handle or tap it to stop a drip**, it means the internal components are severely worn. It's a temporary fix that will inevitably fail completely. These are not minor inconveniences. They are urgent calls for attention. Especially in a villa in The Springs or Jumeirah Park where water pressure systems are often more complex. Ignore them at your peril.
