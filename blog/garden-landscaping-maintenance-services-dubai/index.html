What Makes Dubai Gardens a Unique Challenge?

Maintaining vibrant lawns and healthy plants here isn’t as simple as regular watering and trimming. DEWA water rationing, especially in summer, forces smart irrigation. The heat stresses plants, while occasional sandstorms coat leaves, impacting photosynthesis.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I water my Dubai garden during summer?
Due to evaporation and DEWA restrictions, watering early morning or late evening 3-4 times weekly with drip irrigation is recommended for Dubai gardens.
What types of plants thrive best in Dubai’s climate?
Native and drought-resistant plants like Bougainvillea, Palm trees, and Desert Rose perform well and require less water maintenance.
