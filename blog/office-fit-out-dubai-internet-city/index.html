FAQ: Your Office Fit-Out Questions Answered

What permits are required for an office fit-out in Dubai Internet City?

You need a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from TECOM, DEWA approvals for electrical and water, Dubai Civil Defense (DCD) approval for fire safety, and potentially Trakhees for structural changes. Ignoring these stops your project dead.

How long does a typical office fit-out take in Dubai Internet City?

A standard 100-200 sqm office fit-out can take 4-8 weeks, assuming all approvals are in place and no major structural changes. Delays in permits or material delivery add weeks, sometimes months.

Can I use my existing AC units during the fit-out?

Often, yes, but they need cleaning and balancing. New partitions change airflow dynamics. Without proper re-balancing, hot spots appear, and your AC struggles, leading to premature failure and higher DEWA bills.

What is the biggest mistake companies make during office fit-outs?

Underestimating the complexity of approvals and the need for coordinated trades. Many tech companies focus only on design, forgetting the critical infrastructure. This leads to costly rework and project overruns of 30-50%.

Do I need a separate data cabling contractor for a tech office?

Yes, specialized data cabling is non-negotiable for a tech company. It is not just about running wires. It is about network architecture, future scalability, and minimizing latency. A general electrician often misses these critical details.
