Common Door Problems in Dubai

Dubai's unique environment, particularly the high humidity and temperature fluctuations, can lead to several common door issues:

1. Sticking or Scraping Doors

If your door is difficult to open or close, or if it scrapes against the floor or frame, it is likely due to one of three reasons:

  • Loose Hinges: Over time, the screws holding the hinges can become loose, causing the door to sag.
  • Swelling: Wooden doors can absorb moisture from the humid air, causing them to swell and stick in the frame.
  • Building Settlement: As buildings settle, the door frame can shift slightly out of square.

2. Drafty Doors

A door that doesn't seal properly allows your expensive, air-conditioned air to escape and lets hot, dusty air in. This is usually caused by worn-out weatherstripping or a misaligned door sweep at the bottom.

3. Squeaky Hinges

A squeaky door is a nuisance. While a quick spray of lubricant might offer a temporary fix, persistent squeaking often indicates that the hinge pins are worn or dirty and need proper cleaning and lubrication, or replacement.

4. Sliding Door Issues

Sliding glass doors, common in Dubai balconies and patios, frequently suffer from dirty or damaged tracks, and worn-out rollers, making them heavy and difficult to slide.

Lock Change and Security Upgrades

Your locks are your first line of defense. There are several situations where changing or upgrading your locks is highly recommended:

1. Moving into a New Home

Whether you are renting a new apartment in JVC or buying a villa in Arabian Ranches, you never know who might have a spare key from the previous occupants or contractors. Changing the locks immediately upon moving in is the only way to guarantee your security.

2. Lost or Stolen Keys

If you lose your keys, especially if they were stolen or lost with identifying information, changing the locks is a necessary precaution.

3. Worn or Damaged Locks

Locks endure a lot of wear and tear. If your key is difficult to turn, the lock cylinder is loose, or the mechanism feels "crunchy," it is time for a replacement before you find yourself locked out.

4. Upgrading to Smart Locks

Many Dubai residents are upgrading to smart locks for convenience and enhanced security. Smart locks allow you to enter using a PIN code, fingerprint, or smartphone app, and you can grant temporary access to guests or service providers without handing over a physical key.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How quickly can you change a lock in Dubai?

2. Can you fix a door that scrapes the floor?
Yes, a scraping door is usually caused by loose hinges or a swollen door frame. We can tighten or replace the hinges, or plane the bottom of the door to ensure smooth operation.
3. Do you install smart locks?

Do not try to force the broken piece out, as this can push it further in or damage the cylinder. Call a professional locksmith to extract the key safely.

Yes, we repair sliding doors, including replacing worn-out rollers, realigning the tracks, and fixing or replacing the locking mechanisms.
