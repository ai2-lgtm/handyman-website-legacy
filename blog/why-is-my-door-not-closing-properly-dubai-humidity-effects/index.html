Why Is My Door Not Closing Properly? The Surprising Impact of Dubai Humidity

Door Not Closing Properly? It’s Not Just Your Imagination

Many Dubai residents in communities like Mudon and Remraam struggle with doors that suddenly stick or won’t shut properly. While most blame poor installation or warping, the real culprit is often Dubai’s unique humidity patterns causing wood expansion and frame misalignment.

The Counterintuitive Truth: Humidity Expands Wood, Not Shrinks It

Contrary to popular belief, Dubai’s seasonal humidity—especially in coastal areas like Dubai Creek Harbour—causes wooden doors and frames to absorb moisture and swell, leading to jamming and misalignment. This is especially noticeable during the humid months from October to March when the Gulf’s humidity spikes.

What Went Wrong: Common Misconceptions

  • Assuming Doors Shrink: Many homeowners believe heat causes wood to shrink, but moisture absorption actually expands the door.
  • Ignoring Seasonal Changes: Installing doors without allowing for expansion gaps leads to tight frames in humid conditions.
  • Using Low-Quality Materials: Non-treated wood or inferior laminates are more susceptible to moisture damage.

How Handyman.ae Fixes Door Closing Problems in Dubai

Our technicians assess door frames and humidity exposure, then recommend solutions such as:

  • Planing or Sanding Edges: Carefully trimming swollen edges for smooth operation.
  • Sealing and Waterproofing: Applying moisture-resistant finishes to prevent absorption.
  • Replacing Weather Stripping: Ensuring proper sealing without causing sticking.
  • Adjusting Hinges and Frames: Realigning or reinforcing doorframes to accommodate seasonal movement.

Real-Life Example: Mudon Villa Door Repair

A villa owner in Mudon noticed their main entrance door jamming continuously during winter humidity spikes. Our inspection confirmed swelling caused by untreated wood. We planed the door edges, applied a waterproof sealant, and installed adjustable hinges. The door now closes smoothly year-round.

Pro Tips to Prevent Door Issues in Dubai

  • Use moisture-resistant materials like treated hardwood or uPVC for external doors.
  • Leave expansion gaps when installing new doors.
  • Maintain good indoor humidity levels with AC and dehumidifiers.
  • Regularly inspect and seal wooden doors to prolong lifespan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Dubai’s humidity really affect door functionality?
Yes, increased humidity causes wooden doors to absorb moisture and swell, leading to sticking or jamming.
What materials are best for doors in Dubai’s climate?
Treated hardwood, fiberglass, or uPVC doors resist moisture better and maintain shape in humid conditions.
How often should doors be resealed or maintained to prevent swelling?
Doors should be inspected and resealed annually, especially before and after the humid season.
Can adjusting door hinges solve closing problems related to humidity?
Yes, sometimes realigning or upgrading hinges provides more tolerance for door expansion.
