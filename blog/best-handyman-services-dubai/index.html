What Services Does a Handyman Cover?

The term "handyman" covers a broad spectrum of skills. While specialized trades like major electrical rewiring or complex plumbing overhauls require dedicated technicians, a professional handyman is your go-to expert for a vast array of home maintenance tasks. Here is what you can expect from top-tier handyman services in Dubai:

  • TV Mounting: Secure installation of televisions on all wall types, including gypsum, concrete, and brick, complete with cable concealment.
  • Furniture Assembly: Expert assembly of flat-pack furniture, including IKEA PAX wardrobes, beds, and office desks.
  • Curtain & Blind Installation: Precise fitting of curtain rods, rails, roller blinds, and motorized tracks.
  • Picture & Mirror Hanging: Accurate alignment and secure mounting of heavy mirrors and artwork.
  • Drywall & Gypsum Repair: Patching holes, fixing cracks, and restoring water-damaged walls to a seamless finish.
  • Minor Plumbing & Electrical: Fixing leaky faucets, replacing light fixtures, and installing new electrical outlets.
  • Smart Home Installation: Setting up smart locks, video doorbells, and automated lighting systems.

How to Choose the Best Handyman in Dubai
