What is Actually Happening

You think light is just light.

It is not.

Especially not in Dubai Internet City, where office buildings push the limits of efficiency and comfort.

Old office lighting, typically T8 or T12 fluorescent tubes, operates on a very basic principle. They use an electrical discharge through mercury vapor to produce UV light, which then excites a phosphor coating to emit visible light.

Here is the problem:

Flicker. Fluorescent lights operate on AC power, meaning they turn on and off 100-120 times per second. Your eye cannot see it directly, but your brain processes it. This constant on-off cycle causes eye strain, headaches, and reduces concentration over an 8-hour workday.

Heat. These tubes are inefficient. Up to 70% of the energy consumed by a fluorescent tube is converted into heat, not light. This heat adds to your office's thermal load. Your AC system in Dubai Internet City works harder, consumes more electricity, and struggles to maintain a consistent 22°C ambient temperature. This directly impacts your AC repair and maintenance schedule.

Poor Color Rendering Index (CRI). Old fluorescents have a low CRI. This means colors appear dull, washed out, or even distorted. Imagine reviewing architectural drawings or design mock-ups under yellowed, artificial light. Accuracy suffers. Aesthetic judgment is impaired.

UV Emission. Yes, they emit small amounts of UV. Over time, this can contribute to material degradation – fading documents, artwork, or even office furniture. It is a slow, invisible decay.

Mercury Content. Each tube contains a small amount of mercury. Disposal is an environmental hazard. Breakage is a health risk. It is an outdated, toxic technology still lingering in many offices.

Degradation. Over 24,000 hours of operation, a fluorescent tube dims significantly. Its light output drops by 30-40%. So, your office is getting darker, slower, without you noticing. This is a common issue in offices in Jumeirah Park and JVT where initial fit-outs used budget components.

Modern LED lighting, by contrast, operates on a completely different principle. Semiconductors emit light when current passes through them. No flicker. Minimal heat. High CRI. No mercury. Consistent light output for over 50,000 hours. It is not just a light source; it is a precision tool for your workspace.

Warning Signs

Do not wait for total darkness.

Your office lighting is sending signals.

Are you listening?

  • Flickering Lights: Not a minor annoyance. If your fluorescent tubes blink, buzz, or strobe, it means ballasts are failing. Or wiring is loose. Or the tube itself is dying. This is a direct cause of eye strain and migraines for your team. If you see it, it means your electrical system is under stress.
  • Yellowed or Dim Light: When you walk into your office, does it feel perpetually overcast? Do white walls look off-white? This means your tubes have degraded. They are producing significantly less light than when new. Your office is slowly getting darker.
  • Frequent Bulb Changes: Are your facilities team constantly replacing tubes? This is a clear indicator of poor quality components or an unstable electrical supply. Each replacement means labor cost, material cost, and disruption.
  • Noticeable Heat from Fixtures: If you can feel warmth radiating from your light fixtures, especially recessed ones, that is wasted energy. Your AC is fighting against it. This is a direct contributor to higher cooling bills in Dubai Internet City.
  • Staff Complaints of Eye Strain/Headaches: Direct feedback from your team is critical. If they are reporting discomfort, poor lighting is a primary suspect. This impacts their health and their ability to work effectively.
  • Outdated Fixture Styles: Look up. Are you seeing large, bulky fluorescent troffers from the 90s? Yellowed diffusers? These are not just aesthetically unappealing; they are functionally obsolete. They are energy inefficient and offer poor light distribution.
  • High DEWA Bills (Lighting Component): While not always itemized, a significant portion of your electricity bill is lighting. If your overall electricity consumption feels excessive for your office size in Dubai Internet City, inefficient lighting is a major culprit.

If you recognize even one of these signs, your office is already suffering. The longer you wait, the deeper the pain.
