The Top Maintenance Headaches Disturbing Discovery Gardens Residents

  • Humidity-induced mould and peeling paint in apartments due to poor ventilation and high moisture.
  • Running toilets and leaking taps causing water wastage and inflated DEWA bills.
  • AC units not cooling properly amid Dubai’s summer highs, often due to dirty filters and refrigerant leaks.
  • Electrical circuit tripping from overloaded sockets and faulty wiring in older buildings.

The Risks of Ignoring These Issues

Ignoring maintenance in Discovery Gardens can lead to higher security deposits held by landlords at tenancy end, failed Ejari renewals, and health hazards from mould spores.

For example, mould growth hidden behind gypsum partitions can cause allergies and respiratory issues, while electrical faults increase fire risks.
