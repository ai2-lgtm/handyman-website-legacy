Why Is My AC Leaking Water Inside the House in Dubai? Causes & Solutions

Why Is Your AC Leaking Water Inside Your Dubai Home?

One of the most common and frustrating issues Dubai residents face during the scorching summer months is water leaking from their air conditioning unit inside the house. Despite the cooling relief your AC provides, a leaking unit can cause property damage, promote mold growth, and increase utility bills.

Common Causes of AC Water Leakage in Dubai Homes

Our licensed Handyman.ae technicians, with over 8 years of experience servicing ACs across Dubai’s 46 communities, have identified several typical reasons:

  • Clogged Condensate Drain Line: Dust and debris from Dubai’s dry, sandy environment can block the drain pipe, causing water to back up and leak indoors.
  • Dirty or Frozen Evaporator Coils: Lack of routine cleaning causes ice buildup, which melts and drips inside your unit.
  • Improper Installation or Faulty Drain Pan: Older units or DIY installations may have misaligned or damaged pans that fail to channel water correctly.
  • Low Refrigerant Levels: Leaks in refrigerant lines can cause coils to freeze and drip excess water when thawing.
  • Poor Airflow: Blocked filters or vents reduce airflow, leading to coil freezing and subsequent leaks.

How Dubai’s Climate Amplifies AC Leak Risks

Dubai’s desert climate, with high temperatures and dusty winds, puts extra stress on your AC system. Outdoor units accumulate sand and debris that can clog filters and drain lines if not cleaned regularly. The humid months, especially from May to September, increase condensation inside the system, making proper drainage critical.

Why Quick Action Matters

Ignoring AC leaks can cause damage to ceilings, walls, and flooring, especially in high-rises near Dubai Marina and JBR where moisture can seep between units. It can also lead to mold growth, which is a health hazard in tightly sealed apartments.

  • Schedule professional AC maintenance before and after summer peak seasons.
  • Clean or replace air filters every 1-2 months during heavy usage.
  • Keep outdoor units free from sand and debris.
