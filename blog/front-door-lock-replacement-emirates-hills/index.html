Warning Signs

Your lock is talking to you. Are you listening?

Sticky Key Insertion: If your key does not slide in smoothly, or requires jiggling, the internal pins are misaligned or corroded. This weakens the mechanism. It is a precursor to failure.

Loose Cylinder: Grab the lock cylinder from both sides. Can you wiggle it? Any movement indicates a compromise in its mounting. This makes it easier to pull out or snap off.

Grinding or Catching Mechanism: When you turn the key, does it feel rough? Does it catch? This means internal components are worn or damaged. The lock is failing. It could seize at any moment.

Visible Wear and Tear: Scratches around the keyhole. Dents on the strike plate. These are not just cosmetic. They indicate past attempts at forced entry or heavy use that has weakened the surrounding door material.

Key Duplication Concerns: If you have given out keys to multiple people over the years in your JBR apartment, and cannot account for them all, your key control is compromised. This is a massive security risk.

Outdated Lock Technology: If your lock is more than 7 years old, it likely lacks modern anti-pick, anti-bump, and anti-drill features. Technology evolves. So do intruder methods. Your lock needs to keep pace.

Door Misalignment: Does your door sag? Does it stick when closing? This puts undue stress on the lock bolt and strike plate. A misaligned door means the lock is not engaging fully. It is an open invitation for a kick-in.

These are not minor inconveniences. They are red flags.

Each one signals a vulnerability that needs immediate attention.
