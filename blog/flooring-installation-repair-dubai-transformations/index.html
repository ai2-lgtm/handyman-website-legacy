Common Flooring Issues in Dubai Homes

  • Tile Cracking: Due to shifting sands and temperature fluctuations.
  • Wood Warping and Cupping: Caused by high humidity, especially in older buildings in Deira and Bur Dubai.
  • Grout Discolouration and Mold: Common in kitchens and bathrooms if not sealed properly.
  • Carpet Damage: Stains and wear from dust storms and heavy foot traffic.

How to Maintain Your Floors in Dubai’s Climate

  • Regular cleaning and sealant applications before summer.
  • Immediate repair of cracks or warping to prevent spreading damage.
  • Use rugs and mats in high-traffic or moisture-prone areas.
  • Schedule professional floor inspections annually.
