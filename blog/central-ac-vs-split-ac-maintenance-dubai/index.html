Understanding Your AC System

Central AC Systems

Common in villas and some luxury apartments, central ACs use a network of ducts to distribute cooled air. Their maintenance involves duct cleaning, filter replacement, refrigerant checks, and system calibration. Failure to maintain ducts leads to dust accumulation and mold growth — a health hazard in humid Dubai summers.

Split AC Systems

Split ACs are prevalent in apartments and smaller villas. They cool individual rooms with indoor units linked to an outdoor compressor. Maintaining them requires cleaning coils, ensuring proper refrigerant levels, and clearing debris around outdoor units, especially important in communities like Dubai Hills Estate where dust is ever-present.

Key Differences in Maintenance Requirements

  • Duct Cleaning: Essential for central AC; negligible for split AC.
  • Component Inspection: Split ACs need outdoor unit clearance more often.
  • System Complexity: Central AC maintenance is more technical and best handled by licensed pros.
  • Energy Efficiency: Properly maintained central ACs cool larger areas more efficiently, but split ACs offer room-by-room control.

FAQs on AC Maintenance in Dubai

1. How often should central AC ducts be cleaned in Dubai?

At least once a year, preferably before summer, to prevent dust and mold buildup.

2. Can I clean split AC filters myself?

Yes, cleaning filters every 2-3 weeks is recommended, but deep servicing should be done by professionals.

3. What are signs my AC refrigerant is low?

Reduced cooling, ice formation on coils, and excess energy consumption are common indicators.
