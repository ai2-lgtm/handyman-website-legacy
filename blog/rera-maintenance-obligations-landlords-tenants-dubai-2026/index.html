Understanding RERA Maintenance Obligations

The Real Estate Regulatory Agency (RERA) governs property regulations in Dubai, with a focus on protecting the rights of landlords and tenants. Maintenance obligations are among the core areas regulated to ensure properties remain habitable, safe, and in good condition.

Under RERA, maintenance obligations are split between landlords and tenants to clarify responsibilities and reduce disputes. These rules are set to become even more transparent and enforceable in 2026, making it essential for all parties to stay informed.

The legislation also stresses the importance of timely maintenance and communication. Tenants must promptly report issues, and landlords are required to address them within stipulated timeframes.

Landlord Responsibilities under RERA 2026

For landlords, RERA 2026 maintenance obligations emphasize the duty to preserve the property's structural integrity and essential systems like plumbing, electrical wiring, and air conditioning. Key landlord duties include:

  • Ensuring the property complies with all safety standards and building codes.
  • Promptly repairing major damages or system failures reported by tenants.
  • Maintaining common areas if applicable, including gardens, pools, and walkways.
  • Providing tenants with functional utilities and ensuring proper sanitation.
  • Carrying out preventive maintenance to avoid costly repairs later.

Failure to meet these obligations can lead to penalties from the Dubai Land Department and RERA, as well as disputes that may affect rental agreements.

Tenant Responsibilities under RERA 2026

Tenants also have clear maintenance duties designed to encourage responsible property use and care. According to RERA 2026:

  • Tenants should maintain cleanliness and prevent damage to the property.
  • They must notify landlords immediately when major defects or damages are detected.
  • Tenants are responsible for minor repairs caused by their own negligence or misuse.
  • Any modifications or structural changes require landlord approval before execution.
  • At tenancy end, tenants must return the property in good condition, barring normal wear and tear.

In areas like International City, where many rental communities exist, tenants benefit from understanding these responsibilities to avoid unnecessary charges or disputes.
