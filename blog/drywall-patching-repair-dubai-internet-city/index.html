What Is Actually Happening

Drywall, or gypsum board, is a simple material. Plaster pressed between two sheets of paper. It is strong enough. For vertical loads. For minor impacts.

But it is not invincible.

Especially not in Dubai.

Your apartment in Dubai Internet City, like any other in JBR or Dubai Marina, experiences constant environmental stress.

Humidity is the enemy.

The average relative humidity here hovers between 60% and 90% for months. This moisture is absorbed by the paper facing and the gypsum core.

The drywall expands. Then contracts. Over and over.

This cycle weakens the material. It stresses the tape joints. It exacerbates tiny imperfections.

Think about building settlement. Every new building, even after years, shifts. Micro-movements. These create hairline cracks. Especially around doorframes and windows. These are structural. Not just superficial.

Then there is the internal damage. A leaking AC condensation line. A burst pipe behind the wall. These are silent killers. They saturate the gypsum. The board softens. It sags. It becomes a breeding ground for mold.

Impacts from moving furniture, aggressive cleaning, or even hanging a heavy mirror without proper anchors create punctures. These are not just holes. They expose the interior of the wall cavity. They compromise the vapor barrier. They allow temperature and humidity fluctuations inside the wall.

The drywall you see is a skin. A protective layer. When that skin is broken, the internal structure is vulnerable. And in Dubai, vulnerability means accelerated damage.

Warning Signs

Your walls are talking. Are you listening?

If you see any of these, act. Immediately.

Hairline cracks: Especially around door frames, window sills, or corners. If they reappear after painting, it is not the paint. It is structural movement. Or deeper damage.

Small holes or punctures: From picture hooks, anchors, or accidental impacts. These are not cosmetic. They are open wounds. They invite humidity and pests into the wall cavity.

Bubbling or peeling paint: This is a red flag. It means moisture. Almost always. The paint is losing adhesion because of water. Or extreme humidity. Do not just scrape and repaint. Find the source. It could be a leaking pipe. Or an AC unit's condensation tray overflowing. This is common in apartments in JVT and Jumeirah Park.

Soft spots on the wall: Press on the drywall. Does it feel spongy? Does it give way slightly? This is saturated gypsum. It is rotting. It indicates a significant water leak. Behind the wall. It needs immediate attention.

Discoloration or stains: Yellow, brown, or dark spots on the ceiling or walls. These are water stains. They show where water has been. And where it is still coming from. Do not assume it is old. Assume it is active until proven otherwise.

Loose drywall tape: The paper tape over the joints. If it is peeling, bubbling, or cracking. This suggests movement. Or excessive moisture. The joint is failing. The wall is losing its integrity.

Musty odors near walls: You smell something damp. Something earthy. Especially in a specific area. This is mold. Growing inside your walls. It is a health hazard. And a sign of severe water intrusion. This needs expert painting and repair services immediately.

These are not minor issues. These are symptoms. Of bigger problems brewing. Within the very structure of your apartment in Dubai Internet City.

2. Preparation – Clean and Secure

For holes, we cut out the damaged section. Square or rectangular. Clean edges. This gives us a stable base. For cracks, we widen them slightly. This allows the filler to penetrate. We sand the surrounding area. To ensure adhesion. For soft or moldy areas, we cut back to solid, dry drywall. Then, we clean the area meticulously. Remove all dust. All debris. We apply a bonding agent. This ensures the new material sticks. Firmly.

3. Patching and Filling – Layer by Layer

Small holes (under 5 cm) get mesh patches. Then multiple thin layers of lightweight spackle. Larger holes (up to 15 cm) get a new piece of gypsum board. Cut precisely. Screwed into existing studs. Or supported by wood backing. Taped with fiberglass mesh tape. Over this, we apply joint compound. Not one thick layer. Multiple thin layers. Each layer allowed to dry. Each layer sanded smooth. We feather the edges. Seamlessly. This is critical. It avoids a visible hump or depression. We work in stages. Patience is key. Especially in Dubai's humidity. Each layer cures properly.

4. Sanding and Texturing – The Invisible Repair

After the compound dries completely, we sand. With fine-grit sandpaper. Using dust-extraction tools. The goal is a perfectly flat, smooth surface. Undetectable to the touch. Then, texturing. Most Dubai apartments have a smooth finish. But some might have a light orange peel texture. We match it. Using specialized sprayers or rollers. This is where expertise matters. An incorrectly textured patch stands out like a sore thumb. It ruins the entire wall.

5. Priming and Painting – The Final Touch

We prime the repaired area. With a high-quality primer. This seals the patch. It ensures uniform paint absorption. It prevents flashing. Then, painting. We use premium interior paints. We ask for your existing paint code. Or a paint sample. We apply two coats minimum. Sometimes three. Feathering the edges into the existing paint. The result? A wall that looks brand new. Before painting: This is non-negotiable. Painting over damage just hides it temporarily. It will reappear. Often worse. Always repair and prime all drywall imperfections *before* applying the final paint coats. Our expert painters will insist on this step.

FAQ

What causes common drywall damage in Dubai apartments?

Moving furniture. Kids playing. Art installations gone wrong. But also, hidden leaks from pipes or AC units are major culprits. Minor structural shifts from building settlement also cause hairline cracks.

Can I just paint over small cracks in my Dubai Internet City apartment?

No. Paint hides the symptom. It does not fix the crack. The crack will reappear. Often larger. Often bringing the old paint with it. Patch it correctly first.

How long does a typical drywall repair take?

A small hole (under 10 cm) takes 1-2 hours for patching. Drying time for filler and paint adds 4-6 hours. Larger repairs or water damage can take 1-2 days, depending on layers and complexity.

Will the repaired patch match my existing wall texture and color?

Our process includes feathering and texturing to match. We use a primer. For paint, we ask for your existing paint code or a sample. We aim for an invisible repair, not a noticeable patch.

Is it worth repairing small holes or should I wait for larger damage?

Small holes grow. They invite dust, humidity, and pests into the wall cavity. They weaken the surrounding drywall. Repair small damage immediately. Prevent bigger problems later.
