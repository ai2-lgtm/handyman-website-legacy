How to Fix a Leaking Pipe Under the Sink in Dubai: Quick and Reliable Solutions

The Headache of a Leaking Pipe Under Your Sink in Dubai

A leaking pipe under the kitchen or bathroom sink is one of the most common home maintenance headaches in Dubai apartments and villas alike. Whether you’re in Jumeirah, Business Bay, or Silicon Oasis, ignoring a drip can lead to water damage, increased DEWA bills, and mould growth.

Common Causes of Sink Pipe Leaks in Dubai Homes

  • Worn-Out Washers and Seals: The hot Dubai sun and fluctuating water pressure can degrade rubber washers, resulting in slow drips.
  • Loose Joints and Connectors: Vibrations from water flow or improper installation cause fittings to loosen over time.
  • Corroded Pipes: Older plumbing, especially in buildings over 10 years old like some in Al Barsha, may have corroded metal pipes.
  • Cracked PVC Pipes: Plastic pipes exposed to heat and shifting foundations can crack or split.

Step-by-Step: Quick Fixes You Can Try

  1. Turn Off Water Supply: Locate your apartment or villa’s main water valve to stop the flow before working.
  2. Dry the Area: Use cloth or paper towels to clear water around the leak for better inspection.
  3. Tighten Loose Fittings: Use a wrench to gently tighten pipe connectors; avoid overtightening that may cause damage.
  4. Replace Washers: If the leak is at a joint, remove the fitting and replace old washers with new ones from hardware stores.
  5. Use Pipe Seal Tape: Apply PTFE tape on threaded pipe joints to improve leak resistance.

Preventive Maintenance Tips

  • Schedule an annual plumbing inspection to catch wear early.
  • Avoid chemical drain cleaners that corrode pipes.
  • Keep cabinet interiors dry and well-ventilated.
  • Report any water pressure irregularities to your building management promptly.

Frequently Asked Questions

  1. Why does my sink pipe leak only when I run hot water?
    Heat causes pipes and seals to expand, which can worsen existing cracks or loosen joints.
  2. Can I ignore a slow leak under the sink?
    No. Even slow leaks can cause mould, wood damage, or attract pests in Dubai’s humid climate.
  3. How do I know if the pipe needs replacing?
    Visible corrosion, cracks, or persistent leaks after tightening usually mean replacement is necessary.
  4. Is it safe to fix plumbing myself in Dubai apartments?
    Minor fixes like tightening or washer replacement are ok, but major repairs should be done by licensed plumbers to comply with DEWA and Ejari regulations.
