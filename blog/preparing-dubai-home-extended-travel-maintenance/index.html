Preparing Your Dubai Home for Extended Travel: Essential Maintenance Tips

Preparing Your Dubai Home for Extended Travel: Avoid Headaches with These Proven Maintenance Tips

Dubai’s unique climate and lifestyle create specific challenges when leaving your home vacant for weeks or months. Whether you’re heading abroad during Ramadan or winter, a solid maintenance plan is crucial to protect your property — from sandstorm dust intrusion to AC system readiness.

After 8+ years serving Dubai residents in communities like Jumeirah Lakes Towers and Mudon, Handyman.ae has perfected maintenance routines to eliminate travel-related home headaches.

The Headache: What Happens When You Leave Your Dubai Home Unmaintained While Traveling

  • Sandstorm Dust Build-up: Dubai’s dusty climate, especially during spring sandstorm season, can infiltrate poorly sealed windows and vents, causing long-term damage.
  • AC System Failures: Long inactivity can lead to refrigerant leaks, clogged filters, and electrical faults.
  • Mould and Humidity Damage: Coastal and lagoon communities like The Greens experience high humidity, risking mould growth in bathrooms and kitchens.
  • Security Deposit Risks: Neglected maintenance issues could complicate Ejari renewals or cause disputes if you are renting and subletting.

The Cure: Expert Maintenance Checklist Before Your Extended Trip

  1. Pre-Travel AC System Check and Filter Cleaning: Schedule a full inspection and cleaning; see pre-summer-ac-maintenance-checklist-dubai-homes for detailed tips.
  2. Seal Doors, Windows, and Balcony Access Points: Use weather strips and silicone sealants to prevent dust and moisture entry.
  3. Drain Water from Pipes if Leaving for Several Months: Prevent pipe bursts and leaks common in winter months.
  4. Test Security Systems and Intercoms: Ensure all alarms and CCTV cameras are functional for peace of mind.
  5. Arrange for Regular Property Checks: Hire licensed handymen to conduct weekly inspections, focusing on leaks, mould, and electrical safety.
  6. Inform DEWA and Ejari Authorities: Coordinate your absence to avoid penalties and maintain contract validity.

Additional Tips for Specific Dubai Communities

Dubai Marina and JBR: High humidity and sea breeze require extra vigilance for mould and corrosion.

Arabian Ranches and Emirates Hills: Desert dryness increases dust accumulation; regular air filter replacement is crucial.

Downtown Dubai: High-rise AC systems need professional servicing before long absences to prevent compressor damage.
