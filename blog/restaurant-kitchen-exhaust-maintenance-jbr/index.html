Your exhaust hood sucks these vapors up.

But it does not make them disappear.

The filters catch some of it. A small percentage.

The rest?

It travels through the ductwork.

Through every bend, every seam, every inch of that metal pipe.

The air cools slightly as it moves.

Grease condenses. Turns from vapor back into a sticky liquid.

Then it solidifies.

It clings to the inside of your ducts.

Layer upon layer. Over weeks. Over months.

Warning Signs Your Exhaust System Is Failing

Your kitchen is talking to you. Are you listening?

If you see grease dripping from the exhaust hood filters: This means your filters are saturated. They are no longer filtering effectively. Grease is now bypassing them. It is coating the inside of your visible hood, and accumulating rapidly in your hidden ductwork.

If your kitchen smells smoky, even with the exhaust on: This is a sign of poor airflow. The fan cannot pull air effectively through grease-clogged ducts. Smoke and cooking odors are being pushed back into the kitchen and dining area. Your customers notice this. They will not complain directly. They just will not come back.

If the exhaust fan on the roof sounds louder or is vibrating excessively: The fan motor is struggling. Grease buildup on the fan blades throws it out of balance. It works harder to pull air, drawing more power. This overheats the motor. An overheated motor is a common ignition point for duct fires.

If you notice discolored or greasy patches on the ceiling tiles around the exhaust hood: Grease is escaping the system. It is either leaking from poorly sealed ductwork or being pushed out by insufficient airflow. This is a clear indicator of a severe buildup issue. It is also an aesthetic disaster for your dining area.

If your kitchen is significantly hotter than it should be: Your exhaust system is designed to remove hot air and steam. If it is clogged, it cannot do its job. The heat remains trapped. Your AC works harder, your staff suffer, and your food quality can be affected. This is a common complaint in JVC and Jumeirah Park restaurants with neglected systems.

If your last cleaning certificate is expired or you cannot find it: This is an immediate red flag for Dubai Municipality. No certificate means no recent compliant cleaning. You are immediately exposed to fines and operational suspension.

Do not ignore these warnings. They are not minor inconveniences. They are distress signals from a system on the verge of failure.

This requires opening existing access panels or installing new ones if none exist. We use powerful rotary brushes, specialized scraping tools, and degreasing agents to clean the inside of every duct section. We work from the hood all the way up to the exhaust fan. Every bend, every straight section, is cleaned to bare metal. This eliminates the hidden fire risk. For stubborn or heavily impacted ducts, we deploy powerful steam cleaners.

6. Exhaust Fan Cleaning and Inspection: We meticulously clean the exhaust fan itself. This involves disassembling parts if necessary. Grease is removed from the blades, housing, and motor. How Often / When to Clean Your System

There is no single answer for every restaurant. Dubai Municipality specifies cleaning frequencies based on your cooking volume and type.

High-Volume Cooking (Quarterly Cleaning): If your restaurant operates 16+ hours a day, uses solid fuel cooking (charcoal, wood-fired ovens), or has heavy deep-frying operations, you need cleaning every three months. This includes many busy eateries in Dubai Marina and Palm Jumeirah.

Moderate-Volume Cooking (Semi-Annual Cleaning): Restaurants operating 8-16 hours a day, using gas/electric ovens and grills, fall into this category. Cleaning every six months is mandatory. This is typical for most lunch and dinner service restaurants in JBR and Jumeirah Islands.

Low-Volume Cooking (Annual Cleaning): Cafes, small snack bars, or restaurants with minimal cooking (e.g., reheating, light grilling) might qualify for annual cleaning. However, always confirm with Dubai Municipality. Do not guess. Your license is at stake.

Beyond the official schedule, consider specific events:

Before Peak Season: Clean before the summer heat hits. A clean system performs better under stress.

After Menu Changes: If you introduce new, greasier items, consider an interim clean.

Post-Inspection Correction: If Dubai Municipality flags your system, clean it immediately. Then maintain the new schedule.

Consistency is key. A quarterly clean is far less disruptive and expensive than an emergency clean after a failed inspection or, worse, a fire.

FAQ: Your Burning Questions Answered

How often should a restaurant kitchen exhaust system be cleaned in JBR?

Dubai Municipality regulations mandate cleaning based on cooking volume. High-volume kitchens (24/7 operations, charcoal grills) require quarterly cleaning. Moderate-volume (lunch/dinner) need semi-annual. Low-volume (cafes) can be annual. Do not guess. Check your specific permit.

What are the biggest fire risks in a restaurant kitchen exhaust system?

Grease accumulation is the primary culprit. It is highly flammable. A small spark from the cooktop can ignite grease vapor or solid deposits in the hood, ductwork, or fan. This leads to a catastrophic flash fire. Lack of proper filtration exacerbates this.

What is included in a professional exhaust system cleaning service?

A full-service clean includes meticulous scraping and degreasing of the hood canopy, filters, ductwork from the hood to the fan, the exhaust fan itself, and the rooftop discharge area. Access panels are opened. Everything is cleaned to bare metal. A certificate of compliance is issued.

Why do some exhaust cleaning companies offer cheaper rates?

They often cut corners. They clean only the visible hood, not the hidden ductwork or rooftop fan. This leaves the system dangerously contaminated. It will not pass inspection. It is a false economy that risks your entire business. Demand a full, top-to-bottom clean.

Does exhaust cleaning impact my restaurant's ventilation and AC performance?

Absolutely. A grease-clogged exhaust system significantly reduces airflow. This makes your kitchen hotter, reduces the effectiveness of your AC, and pushes smoke and odors back into the dining area. Your AC works harder, consuming more power, but achieves less. A clean system improves efficiency dramatically.

What happens if a restaurant fails a Dubai Municipality exhaust system inspection?

Immediate consequences. Fines are levied. You may be ordered to cease operations until the system is brought into compliance. Repeat offenses can lead to license suspension or revocation. It is a severe risk to your business continuity. Do not gamble with compliance.
