How to Fix a Blocked Kitchen Sink in Dubai: Expert DIY and Professional Tips

Introduction: The Common Blocked Kitchen Sink Problem in Dubai Homes

Blocked kitchen sinks are one of the most frequent plumbing headaches in Dubai, especially in areas like Discovery Gardens, Mirdif, and International City where older properties face wear and tear. With Dubai’s hard water and occasional DEWA water quality issues, kitchen drainage requires attention to prevent bigger problems like sewage backflow or mould.

Having completed over 15,000+ plumbing jobs since 2016, Handyman.ae provides this step-by-step guide to help you resolve minor blockages safely and know when professional help is essential.

Common Causes of Kitchen Sink Blockages in Dubai

  • Food debris and grease buildup in pipes
  • Accumulation of soap scum mixed with hard water minerals
  • Foreign objects accidentally dropped down the drain
  • Clogged traps and P-trap due to sediment or corrosion
  • Structural pipe damage from Dubai’s shifting ground in some communities like Al Quoz

Step-by-Step Guide: How to Fix a Blocked Kitchen Sink

Step 1: Remove Standing Water and Clear Visible Debris

Use a small container or cup to scoop out water. Remove the drain stopper or strainer carefully and clear out any visible food or debris.

Step 2: Use a Plunger Specifically Designed for Sinks

Fill the sink partly with water to cover the plunger’s cup. Create a tight seal and plunge vigorously 15-20 times. Avoid excessive force to prevent pipe damage.

Step 3: Apply a Baking Soda and Vinegar Mixture

Pour half a cup of baking soda down the drain, followed by half a cup of white vinegar. Cover the drain and let it fizz for 30 minutes. Flush with hot water supplied by DEWA to help clear grease.

Step 4: Check and Clean the P-trap

Place a bucket underneath the P-trap (the curved pipe beneath the sink). Unscrew the connectors carefully, clean out trapped debris, and reassemble. This step often solves stubborn clogs.

Step 5: Use a Drain Snake for Deeper Blockages

If the blockage persists, a manual drain snake can reach deeper traps and bends. Avoid chemical drain cleaners—they damage pipes and violate Dubai Municipality standards.

Preventing Future Kitchen Sink Blockages in Dubai

  • Dispose food scraps in trash or compost, not down the drain
  • Use drain strainers to catch debris
  • Flush drains with hot water weekly to clear grease
  • Schedule periodic plumbing inspections, especially in older buildings
