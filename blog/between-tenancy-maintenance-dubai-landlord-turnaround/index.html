The Dubai Climate Factor: Accelerating Wear and Tear

Dubai's unique environment presents significant challenges for property maintenance, particularly between tenancies. The relentless heat, often exceeding 40°C, causes materials to expand and contract, leading to cracks in walls and ceilings, and accelerating the degradation of sealants and adhesives. High humidity levels foster mould and mildew growth, especially in bathrooms and kitchens, compromising air quality and aesthetics. The pervasive dust from construction and desert winds infiltrates every crevice, clogging AC filters and grilles, and scratching surfaces. Furthermore, the corrosive salt air, particularly near coastal areas, rusts metal fixtures and discolours external paintwork. Handyman.ae understands these specific pressures, offering tailored painting services and comprehensive solutions to mitigate their impact, ensuring your property remains in prime condition for new tenants.
