Bathroom Renovation Cost in Dubai 2026

A bathroom renovation is one of the most impactful upgrades you can make to your Dubai home. Whether you are modernizing an outdated apartment in Dubai Marina or upgrading a luxury villa in Emirates Hills, a beautifully designed bathroom significantly increases your property's value and your daily comfort.

However, budgeting for a renovation can be confusing. Prices vary wildly depending on the materials you choose, the size of the space, and the contractor you hire. At Handyman.ae, we believe in complete transparency. In this comprehensive 2026 guide, we break down the true costs of bathroom renovations in Dubai, so you know exactly what to expect.

To help you budget accurately, we have categorized renovations into three tiers:

1. Budget / Refresh Renovation

This tier is ideal for landlords looking to refresh a rental property or homeowners on a strict budget. It usually involves keeping the existing plumbing layout to save money.

  • Materials: Standard ceramic tiles (e.g., from RAK Ceramics), basic sanitary ware (e.g., Milano), and a simple vanity unit.
  • Scope: Retiling over existing tiles (if space permits), replacing the toilet and sink, and updating the showerhead and faucets.

2. Mid-Range / Standard Renovation

This is the most common type of renovation for homeowners in Dubai. It involves a complete strip-down of the bathroom and allows for minor layout changes.

  • Materials: Porcelain or mid-range marble-effect tiles, branded sanitary ware (e.g., Grohe, Kohler), a custom or high-quality pre-made vanity, and a frameless glass shower enclosure.
  • Scope: Complete demolition, new waterproofing, new plumbing pipes, full retiling, and installation of a walk-in shower (replacing an old bathtub).

3. Luxury / High-End Renovation

For those looking to create a spa-like retreat, the sky is the limit. This tier involves premium materials and complex installations.

  • Materials: Natural stone (marble or travertine) or large-format porcelain slabs, high-end European fixtures (e.g., Villeroy & Boch, Hansgrohe), smart toilets, freestanding bathtubs, and custom LED lighting.
  • Scope: Major layout changes, custom niches, underfloor heating, and bespoke cabinetry.

Hidden Costs to Watch Out For

When planning your budget, be sure to account for these often-overlooked expenses:

  • NOC and Approvals: Most buildings in Dubai require a No Objection Certificate (NOC) before work begins. Some developers charge a non-refundable fee or require a refundable security deposit (contact us for a quote).
  • Waterproofing: Never cut corners here. Proper waterproofing is essential in Dubai apartments to prevent leaks into the unit below. If the contractor finds severe water damage after demolition, repairing the concrete slab will add to the cost.
  • Plumbing Relocation: Moving a toilet or shower drain even a few feet requires breaking into the concrete floor and rerouting pipes, which significantly increases labor costs.

How to Save Money on Your Bathroom Renovation

If you want to achieve a high-end look without the luxury price tag, consider these tips:

  1. Keep the Layout: As mentioned, moving plumbing fixtures is expensive. Keep the toilet, sink, and shower in their original locations to save thousands of dirhams.
  2. Choose Porcelain Over Marble: Natural marble is beautiful but expensive and requires high maintenance. Modern porcelain tiles mimic the look of marble perfectly at a fraction of the cost.
  3. Refinish Instead of Replace: If your bathtub is in good structural condition but looks dated, consider having it professionally reglazed instead of replacing it.
  4. Buy Your Own Fixtures: While contractors get trade discounts on raw materials (cement, pipes), you can often find great deals on tiles and sanitary ware during sales at stores like Danube Home, RAK Ceramics, or Dragon Mart.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a full bathroom renovation cost in Dubai?
A full bathroom renovation in Dubai typically costs vary depending on a standard-sized bathroom. Luxury renovations with high-end materials can vary significantly.
How long does it take to renovate a bathroom in Dubai?
A complete bathroom remodel usually takes between 10 to 14 days, depending on the scope of work, availability of materials, and building management approvals (NOC).
Do I need an NOC from my building management to renovate my bathroom?
Yes, almost all apartment buildings and villa communities in Dubai require a No Objection Certificate (NOC) before any major renovation work, especially if it involves plumbing or structural changes.
Can I just replace the tiles without doing a full renovation?
Yes, partial renovations are common. Replacing floor and wall tiles typically costs between contact us for a quote and contact us for a quote depending on the size of the bathroom and the cost of the tiles.
Is it cheaper to buy the materials myself?
Buying materials yourself gives you more control over the budget, but contractors often get trade discounts. A hybrid approach—where you buy the visible fixtures (tiles, vanity) and the contractor supplies the raw materials (cement, pipes)—is usually the most cost-effective.
