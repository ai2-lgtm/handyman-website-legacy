Common Garage Door Problems in Dubai Villas

Understanding typical garage door issues can help you identify when it’s time to seek professional repair or maintenance. Here are some of the most frequent problems villa owners face:

1. Broken Springs

Garage door springs bear the weight of the door and allow smooth opening and closing. These springs can snap due to metal fatigue or extreme weather conditions. A broken spring can cause the door to become heavy and unsafe to operate.

2. Faulty Garage Door Opener

Issues with the motor or remote controls can prevent your garage door from responding properly. Electronics can degrade over time, and power surges or wiring faults may also cause malfunctions.

3. Misaligned or Damaged Tracks

If the tracks that guide your garage door become bent or misaligned, the door may jam or move unevenly, leading to increased wear and possible damage to other components.

4. Noisy Operation

Squeaking, grinding, or banging noises indicate that parts need lubrication, adjustment, or replacement. Ignoring these sounds can lead to bigger problems down the line.

5. Sensor and Safety Issues

Modern garage doors have safety sensors to prevent accidents. Faulty sensors or wiring can cause the door to reverse unexpectedly or fail to close fully.
