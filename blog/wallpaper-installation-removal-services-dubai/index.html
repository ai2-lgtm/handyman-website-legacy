The Wallpaper Headache: Common Challenges in Dubai

  • Humidity and Temperature: Dubai’s high humidity causes wallpaper glue to weaken, leading to peeling.
  • Surface Preparation Problems: Walls in older buildings like Discovery Gardens often have plaster cracks or dust that prevent proper adhesion.
  • Incorrect Removal Techniques: DIY removal can damage drywall or leave sticky residues.
  • Time-Consuming: Wallpapering is labor-intensive, especially in high-rise apartments such as those in Downtown Dubai.

Maintaining Wallpaper in Dubai’s Climate

  • Avoid direct water contact; wipe with dry cloth or mild detergent only.
  • Control indoor humidity using DEWA-approved air conditioning and dehumidifiers.
  • Inspect edges regularly for lifting and call professionals for repairs.

Frequently Asked Questions About Wallpaper Installation and Removal in Dubai

  • Q: How long does wallpaper last in Dubai’s humid conditions?
    A: With professional installation and proper maintenance, wallpaper can last 5-10 years even in humid areas.
  • Q: Can I install wallpaper over painted walls?
    A: Yes, but the paint must be fully cured and the surface smooth and clean for best adhesion.
  • Q: Is wallpaper removal messy?
    A: Professional removal minimizes mess and damage. DIY attempts often cause wall damage and residue.
  • Q: Can wallpaper be used in bathrooms and kitchens?
    A: Yes, but moisture-resistant or vinyl wallpapers are recommended along with proper ventilation.
