End of Tenancy Maintenance Checklist Dubai — Protect Your Security Deposit

Handing over your Dubai property at the end of your tenancy can be stressful, especially when it comes to securing your security deposit. Landlords and management companies in areas like Jumeirah Beach Residence, Downtown Dubai, and Dubai Marina are strict with property conditions. After completing 15,000+ jobs across 46 Dubai communities, Handyman.ae knows exactly what it takes to pass the final inspection smoothly.

Why End of Tenancy Maintenance Matters in Dubai

Dubai’s climate, with its intense summer heat and occasional dust storms, accelerates wear and tear on properties. This means tenants often face deductions for issues like air conditioning faults, water leaks, or sand-damaged windows. DEWA and Ejari regulations also require properties to be in good standing before handover.

Failing to address these common maintenance points can result in disputes and lost deposits. Our checklist ensures you leave your Dubai home in excellent condition, whether it's a villa in Arabian Ranches or an apartment in Business Bay.

Pre-Move-Out Cleaning and Repairs Checklist

  • Air Conditioning: Ensure your AC units are functioning perfectly. Dubai's summer heat makes this a top priority. Refer to our pre-summer AC maintenance checklist for tips.
  • Plumbing: Fix leaky faucets, running toilets, and blocked drains. Water damage can cause major deposit deductions. Check our complete guide on professional plumbing services for trusted solutions.
  • Electrical Fixtures: Replace burnt-out bulbs, repair faulty switches, and ensure circuit breakers aren’t tripping. Safety first, especially in high-rises like Burj Khalifa or Marina Towers.
  • Walls and Paint: Touch up scuffs and holes. Sandstorms and humidity in Dubai can cause paint peeling, so a fresh coat may be necessary. Explore our professional painting services in Dubai for expert help.
  • Windows and Doors: Clean glass thoroughly, lubricate hinges, and repair seals to prevent DEWA issues and security deposit disputes.
  • Kitchen Appliances: Test ovens, microwaves, and built-in units. Ensure all are clean and in working order.
  • Flooring: Repair cracked tiles, remove stains from carpets or wooden flooring, especially in humid communities like JVC.
  • General Cleaning: Deep clean bathrooms, kitchens, and living areas. Dubai landlords expect spotless homes.

Timing Is Everything: When to Schedule Maintenance

Plan your maintenance a week or two before handover to allow time for any unforeseen repairs. Booking with Handyman.ae early ensures same-day service availability, even during Dubai’s peak moving seasons.

Don’t wait until the last minute—landlords in gated communities like Emirates Hills or The Springs enforce strict handover standards. A timely, thorough maintenance service means avoiding deductions and ensuring your deposit returns in full.
