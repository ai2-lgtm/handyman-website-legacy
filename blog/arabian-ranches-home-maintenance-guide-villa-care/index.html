What Most People Don’t Know About Arabian Ranches Villa Maintenance

Many homeowners underestimate the subtle but critical maintenance areas that affect property value and comfort:

  • Irrigation System Calibration: Many irrigation systems are not optimized for Dubai’s sandy soil and varying salinity levels, leading to overwatering or under-watering plants.
  • AC System Overuse & Maintenance: The desert climate pushes air conditioning systems hard; neglect leads to higher energy bills and system failures.
  • Exterior Wall and Roof Care: The sandy environment causes surface wear; regular painting and waterproofing preserve structure integrity.
  • Evolving Ejari and DEWA Compliance: Documentation requirements are strict for villa owners renting properties in Arabian Ranches, mandating up-to-date maintenance records.

Secret Maintenance Tips for Arabian Ranches Villas

  1. Schedule AC servicing twice a year: Before summer and winter, to optimize efficiency and comply with DEWA.
  2. Inspect irrigation monthly: Adjust settings seasonally to prevent overuse of water—a precious resource in Dubai.
  3. Maintain exterior paint: Repaint every 3-4 years using weatherproof Dubai-approved materials.
  4. Book annual waterproofing inspections: Prevent balcony and roof leaks before they escalate.
