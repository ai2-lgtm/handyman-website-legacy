First Time Renter in Dubai? Essential Home Maintenance Tips You Need to Know

Welcome to Dubai Renting: Why Maintenance Matters More Than You Think

Renting your first home in Dubai—whether in bustling Business Bay, family-friendly Jumeirah, or scenic Mudon—comes with exciting opportunities and responsibilities. Beyond signing the lease and registering Ejari, maintaining your property protects your security deposit and ensures a comfortable living experience.

Having completed 15,000+ jobs across Dubai’s 46 communities, Handyman.ae shares expert insights specific to Dubai’s climate, DEWA protocols, and landlord expectations.

Understand Your Ejari and Security Deposit Responsibilities

Ejari registration requires the property to be in good condition. Landlords expect tenants to maintain the home, addressing minor repairs promptly to avoid deductions from the security deposit at tenancy end.

Common issues that cause disputes include:

  • Air conditioning malfunctions due to lack of servicing
  • Water leaks or running toilets
  • Peeling paint or damaged walls
  • Electrical faults or flickering lights

Essential Maintenance Tips for First-Time Renters

1. Schedule AC Servicing Before Summer

Dubai’s summers are unforgiving, with temperatures often above 45°C. Pre-summer AC checks prevent breakdowns and high DEWA bills. See our detailed pre-summer AC maintenance checklist for actionable tips.

2. Inspect Plumbing Regularly

Check taps, toilets, and drains for leaks or blockages. Early fixes avoid water damage and mold growth, common in humid areas like Dubai Marina and JBR.

3. Maintain Electrical Safety

Be alert to flickering lights or tripping circuits. Faulty wiring poses fire risks and can invalidate your insurance. Our electrical safety tips for Dubai homes are essential reading.

4. Keep Your Home Clean and Pest-Free

Sandstorms bring dust that settles indoors, and Dubai’s climate attracts pests. Regular deep cleaning, including AC duct cleaning, helps maintain air quality.

5. Report Issues Promptly

Delaying repairs leads to bigger problems and higher costs. Handyman.ae offers 24/7 emergency service, so you’re never left waiting.

Preparing for End-of-Tenancy Inspection

Plan ahead with a checklist that includes:

  • Fixing running toilets and dripping taps
  • Repairing door handles and locks
  • Touching up paint damage
  • Cleaning AC filters and vents

FAQs for First-Time Renters in Dubai

Do I have to fix damages caused by previous tenants?

No. Always report existing damages to your landlord and document them before moving in to avoid liabilities.

How often should I service my AC as a tenant?

At least twice a year — before summer and after — to keep it efficient and DEWA-friendly.

Who is responsible for home maintenance during tenancy?

Tenants handle minor repairs; landlords usually cover major structural issues. Review your tenancy contract carefully.
