Common Dubai-Specific Challenges in Glass Installation

Dubai’s climate and urban environment bring unique challenges:

  • Thermal Expansion: Glass expands with heat; poor installation leads to cracks.
  • Sand and Dust Intrusion: Requires tight seals especially in communities near desert edges like Al Qudra.
  • Building Codes: Compliance with Dubai Municipality’s regulations for high-rises and villas.
