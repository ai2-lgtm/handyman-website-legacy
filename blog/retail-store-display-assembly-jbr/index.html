Warning Signs: Is Your Display Failing?

Look around your JBR store. Be critical. Be honest.

These are not "character" marks. These are red flags.

  • Wobbly Shelves: If you can push a shelf with your finger and it visibly moves, it's not secure. This is a primary indicator of improper joint connection or insufficient support.
  • Crooked Panels/Doors: Display cabinet doors that don't align. Panels that have noticeable gaps. This indicates the frame was assembled out of square, or hinges were installed unevenly.
  • Stripped Screws/Visible Damage: See screws that are angled, or have circular marks around them where the screwdriver slipped? This means the fastener was over-torqued or the wrong bit was used. It compromises the hold.
  • Squeaking or Groaning Sounds: When customers interact with the display – sliding a hanger, opening a drawer – do you hear unsettling noises? This is friction from misaligned parts or stress on weak joints.
  • Uneven Weight Distribution: Are heavier products always placed on the bottom because the top shelves feel flimsy? This is a tacit admission that you don't trust your own display's integrity.
  • Missing Hardware: Find spare screws or brackets that were "left over" after assembly? They weren't spare. They were missed. Your unit is incomplete and structurally compromised.
  • Paint/Finish Chipping Near Joints: This suggests excessive force was used during assembly, or the parts aren't sitting flush, causing constant rubbing and wear.
  • Displays Leaning: Stand back. Are your tall units perfectly vertical? Or do they have a slight lean? This is a major stability issue, especially if not anchored to a wall. We see this often in Jumeirah Park villas with freestanding wardrobes.

If you see any of these signs, your display is not "settling in." It's failing.

It's costing you money and reputation.
