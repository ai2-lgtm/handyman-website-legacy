What Happens When You Ignore a Leaking Shower in Dubai

By Dubai's handyman — Handyman.ae

A small drip in your Dubai shower is rarely just a small drip. In an apartment building stacked on shared waste stacks and tiled with cement-bedded ceramics, a leaking shower can quietly turn into a five-figure repair bill, a neighbour dispute, and a permanent mould problem inside weeks.

1. Mould inside the shower wall — fast

Dubai humidity sits between 55% and 90% most of the year. Once water saturates grout and the cement bed beneath your tiles, mould can begin colonising within 24–48 hours. By the time you smell it, it is already inside the wall cavity, on the gypsum board, and behind the skirting tiles.

2. Damage to the unit below you

Most Dubai apartments share a concrete slab with the unit beneath. A persistent leak will eventually penetrate the waterproof membrane, run down the slab, and emerge as ceiling stains in your downstairs neighbour's living room or bedroom. RERA and most building managements treat this as an urgent maintenance issue — and the cost of repair to both apartments is normally pushed to whichever owner failed to act.

3. The repair scope keeps growing

A fresh leak might mean a new mixer cartridge or a re-grout. A two-month-old leak typically means stripping the shower wall, replacing waterproofing, re-tiling, and patching the ceiling below. We have seen jobs grow from a 200 AED cartridge swap to a 12,000 AED rebuild in a single quarter.

4. Your AMC and tenancy contract may not cover it

Most Dubai annual maintenance contracts and tenancy agreements exclude consequential damage from “known but ignored” issues. If you reported the leak and waited, you are usually still covered. If you ignored it, the landlord or AMC provider can refuse the claim.

What to do today

  1. Photograph the leak and timestamp it.
  2. Email your landlord or building management today — not by WhatsApp.
  3. Book a licensed plumber to investigate and quote.
  4. Keep the bathroom dry and ventilated until the fix is done.
