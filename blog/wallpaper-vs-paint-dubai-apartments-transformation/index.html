Wallpaper vs Paint for Dubai Apartments — Which Transformed Our Client’s Home Best?

When a Jumeirah Lakes Towers (JLT) apartment owner contacted Handyman.ae to refresh their living space, the debate began: Should they choose wallpaper or paint? Both options have passionate advocates, but the decision is not as simple as it looks.

Before: The Challenges of Dubai Apartments

Our client’s apartment suffered from fading paint after intense summer sun, humidity-induced peeling, and the monotony of plain white walls common in many JLT buildings. The landlord wanted a modern look but also something durable enough to withstand Dubai’s coastal air and occasional sandstorms.

Exploring Wallpaper: Style with Caveats

  • Pros: Wallpaper offers rich textures, patterns, and the ability to create accent walls that paint cannot replicate. It’s ideal for feature walls in living rooms or bedrooms.
  • Cons: Humidity in Dubai can cause peeling if wallpapers are not installed with moisture-proof adhesives. Removal is tricky, often requiring professional help to avoid damage.

Exploring Paint: Flexibility and Simplicity

  • Pros: Paint is cost-effective, easy to refresh, and available in countless colors and finishes. Modern Dubai-approved paints have UV resistance to minimize fading.
  • Cons: Repainting requires more frequent upkeep in Dubai’s climate, especially in sun-exposed rooms.

The Transformation Journey with Handyman.ae

Our licensed, insured technicians started with a moisture assessment to avoid future peeling or blistering. For the feature wall, we applied a textured wallpaper designed for humid climates, sourced from Dubai’s Al Quoz design district.

Every other wall received a premium UV-resistant paint application, sealed with an anti-fungal coat to prevent mould growth common in coastal apartments like Dubai Marina.

After: The Results Speak

The client’s apartment exuded warmth and sophistication. The wallpaper added character to the living room without peeling after two months of sea breeze exposure. The paint remained vibrant with minimal touch-ups.

We also provided a maintenance guide to help the client keep the walls pristine, which included professional cleaning schedules aligned with Dubai’s sandstorm seasons.

Maintenance Tips for Dubai Apartments

  • For wallpaper, avoid direct moisture exposure. Use dehumidifiers during winter months.
  • For paint, schedule professional inspections annually—especially important for high-rise buildings in Downtown Dubai where sun intensity is extreme.
  • Inspect and clean walls after summer to prevent dust and sand accumulation.
  • Consider our Annual Maintenance Contract for seamless upkeep.
