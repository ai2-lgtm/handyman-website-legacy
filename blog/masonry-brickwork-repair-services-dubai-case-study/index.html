The villa’s masonry wall was exposed to the humid sea breeze and occasional rain, accelerating deterioration. Cracks were widening, and some bricks had loosened, exposing rebar rust and risking collapse. Immediate repairs were needed to comply with Ejari lease requirements and avoid security deposit issues.

Step-by-Step Masonry and Brickwork Repair Process

  1. Detailed Inspection: Our experienced team inspected the entire wall, checking brick alignment, mortar condition, and underlying structure.
  2. Material Selection: We recommended Dubai-grade cement and weather-resistant bricks designed for coastal environments.
  3. Demolition of Damaged Sections: Loose bricks and crumbling mortar were carefully removed to prepare for new work.
  4. Reinforcement: Where rebar was rusted, we treated and replaced it to strengthen the structure.
  5. Bricklaying and Mortaring: Skilled masons re-laid bricks ensuring even spacing and strong bonding, matching existing patterns.
  6. Finishing: We applied a waterproof sealant and cleaned the wall to restore its original look.

Common Masonry Problems in Dubai Properties

Whether you own a villa in Arabian Ranches or an apartment in Business Bay, masonry issues can arise from:

  • Saltwater exposure causing brick surface erosion.
  • Foundation shifts due to sandy soil.
  • Improper mortar mixes causing weak joints.
  • Damage from sandstorms or accidental impacts.

Ignoring these can lead to mold, water leaks, and even structural failure.

Maintenance Tips to Prevent Masonry Damage

  • Schedule a masonry inspection every 2-3 years.
  • Clean brick surfaces regularly to remove salt deposits.
  • Seal bricks with waterproof treatments.
  • Fix cracks immediately to prevent water ingress.
