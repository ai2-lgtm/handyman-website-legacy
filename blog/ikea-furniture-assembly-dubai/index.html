IKEA Furniture Assembly Service in Dubai

Moving into a new apartment in Dubai or simply upgrading your living room often involves a trip to IKEA. While their flat-pack furniture is stylish and affordable, the assembly process can quickly turn into a frustrating, time-consuming ordeal. From deciphering confusing instruction manuals to realizing you are missing a crucial screw, DIY furniture assembly is not for everyone.

That is where Handyman.ae comes in. As one of the best handyman services in Dubai, we specialize in fast, professional, and stress-free IKEA furniture assembly. Whether you need a simple MALM dresser put together or a complex PAX wardrobe system installed, our experienced team is ready to help.

Why Hire a Professional for IKEA Furniture Assembly?

While IKEA designs its furniture to be assembled by the consumer, there are several compelling reasons to hire a professional handyman in Dubai:

1. Save Time and Frustration

What might take you an entire weekend to assemble can often be completed by a professional in just a few hours. Our handymen assemble IKEA furniture daily; they know the quirks of the products and can navigate the instructions with ease, saving you hours of frustration.

2. Proper Tools and Equipment

The tiny Allen key provided in the IKEA box is rarely enough for a sturdy build. Professional handymen arrive with a full toolkit, including power drills, levels, stud finders, and specialized bits. This ensures that screws are tightened correctly and the furniture is structurally sound.

3. Safe Wall Mounting

Many IKEA items, such as the KALLAX shelving units, BESTÅ TV benches, and PAX wardrobes, must be securely anchored to the wall to prevent tipping. Dubai apartments often have different wall types (drywall vs. concrete). A professional knows exactly which anchors to use to ensure your furniture is safely secured, protecting your family and pets.

4. Avoid Costly Mistakes

One wrong move—like using a screw that is too long or assembling a piece backward—can damage the veneer or compromise the structural integrity of the furniture. Hiring an expert eliminates the risk of ruining your new purchase.
