International City Home Maintenance: 10 Rapid Fire Insights for Budget-Conscious Dubai Residents

International City Home Maintenance: Quick Tips for Every Apartment and Villa

International City is a vibrant, budget-friendly community attracting residents seeking value without compromising on lifestyle. However, affordable living comes with its own maintenance challenges that, if left unaddressed, can escalate rapidly.

Handyman.ae has been helping International City residents since 2016 with licensed technicians completing thousands of jobs in this community. Here are 10 rapid-fire insights tailored to your home maintenance requirements.

1. Prioritize AC Filter Cleaning Every 3 Months

Dubai’s dusty environment clogs AC filters quickly, especially in International City where older buildings have less sealed windows. Regular cleaning maintains cooling efficiency and reduces DEWA bills.

2. Check for Leaks Behind Kitchen Cabinets

Plumbing leaks are common in International City apartments due to older piping. Early detection prevents water damage and mould growth.

3. Schedule Annual Water Tank Cleaning

Many International City buildings rely on rooftop water tanks that accumulate sediment. Clean tanks ensure potable water and compliance with Ejari lease requirements.

4. Inspect Electrical Outlets for Overloading

Due to smaller unit sizes, residents often use multiple extension cords. This can cause electrical circuit tripping and pose fire risks.

5. Use DEWA-Approved LED Lighting

Switching to energy-efficient lighting helps reduce utility bills and meets Dubai’s sustainability goals.

6. Test Smoke Alarms and Fire Safety Equipment

Fire safety compliance is critical in high-density communities like International City. Replace batteries regularly and ensure alarms are functional.

7. Repair Cracked Tiles Promptly

Ignoring cracked or loose tiles can cause trips and water ingress leading to structural damage in buildings.

8. Clean Balcony Drains Before Rainy Season

Blocked drains lead to water pooling and potential flooding during Dubai’s rare but intense rains.

9. Maintain Curtains and Blinds

Dust accumulates quickly in International City apartments. Use professional curtain cleaning and repair services for longevity.

10. Keep Documentation for Ejari and Landlord Inspections

Maintain records of all maintenance and repairs. This helps smooth Ejari renewals and security deposit returns.
