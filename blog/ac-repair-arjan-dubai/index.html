Common AC Problems in Arjan Homes

Even the most robust air conditioning systems can encounter problems, especially under the constant demand placed upon them in Dubai's hot climate. Identifying these common issues early can help you understand when to call for professional AC repair in Arjan Dubai. One of the most frequent problems we encounter is insufficient cooling. This could be due to low refrigerant levels, a dirty condenser coil, a faulty compressor, or even a thermostat malfunction. A lack of proper cooling not only makes your space uncomfortable but also signals that your unit is working harder than it should, leading to higher energy consumption.

Another prevalent issue is unusual noises emanating from the AC unit. Grinding sounds often point to a problem with the motor bearings, while a hissing sound can indicate a refrigerant leak. Banging noises might suggest a loose part within the system. Any strange sound should be investigated promptly by a professional to prevent further damage. Additionally, water leaks are a common complaint. These can stem from a clogged condensate drain line, a cracked drain pan, or even a frozen evaporator coil. Addressing these issues quickly is important to prevent water damage to your property and to ensure the proper functioning of your AC system.
