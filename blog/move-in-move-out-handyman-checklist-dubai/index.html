Move-in/Move-out Handyman Checklist Dubai: Secure Your Deposit

Moving is stressful enough without the added anxiety of losing your security deposit or moving into a new home with hidden maintenance issues. In Dubai's fast-paced rental market, a smooth transition requires careful planning and attention to detail. Whether you are handing over the keys to your old landlord or stepping into your new villa, this comprehensive move-in/move-out handyman checklist from Handyman.ae will ensure you are fully prepared.

The Move-Out Checklist: Getting Your Deposit Back

Your primary goal when moving out is to return the property in the same condition you received it, minus "normal wear and tear." Landlords in Dubai are notoriously strict during final inspections, and any damage can result in significant deductions from your security deposit (usually 5% of the annual rent).

1. Wall Repairs and Painting

This is the most common reason for deposit deductions. If you hung pictures, mounted a TV, or painted an accent wall, you need to restore the walls.

  • Fill Holes: Remove all nails, screws, and wall plugs. Fill the holes with spackle or joint compound and sand them smooth.
  • Repainting: Most contracts stipulate returning the walls to their original color (usually off-white). Even if you didn't change the color, significant scuffs or stains may require a fresh coat of paint.

2. Plumbing and Fixtures

Ensure all water fixtures are functioning correctly and are free of leaks.

  • Check for Leaks: Inspect under sinks and around toilets for any signs of dripping water.
  • Unclog Drains: Ensure sinks and showers drain quickly. Remove any hair or debris.
  • Replace Broken Fixtures: If you broke a showerhead or cracked a toilet seat, replace it before the inspection.

3. Electrical and Lighting

A fully functioning electrical system is expected upon handover.

  • Replace Bulbs: Ensure every light fixture has a working bulb.
  • Check Outlets: Test all sockets to ensure they are providing power.
  • Appliance Check: If the property came with appliances (white goods), ensure they are clean and in working order.

4. General Repairs

Walk through the property and look for any other damage you may have caused.

  • Doors and Cabinets: Fix loose hinges, broken handles, or doors that don't close properly.
  • Flooring: Repair any deep scratches in wood flooring or cracked tiles.
  • Deep Cleaning: While not strictly a handyman task, a professional deep cleaning is essential before the final handover.

The Move-In Checklist: Starting Fresh

When you move into a new property, your goal is to document its current condition and address any immediate maintenance needs before you unpack.

1. The Snagging List

Before moving your furniture in, conduct a thorough inspection and create a "snagging list" of any existing damage. Take clear photos and videos and email them to your landlord or property manager immediately. This protects you from being blamed for pre-existing issues when you eventually move out.

2. AC Inspection

In Dubai, a functioning AC is non-negotiable. Turn on the AC in every room and ensure it is cooling effectively. Check the filters; if they are dirty, request that the landlord clean or replace them.

3. Plumbing Check

Test every faucet, shower, and toilet. Look for leaks, check the water pressure, and ensure the hot water heater is functioning correctly.

4. Security and Locks

For your peace of mind, it is highly recommended to change the locks on your new home. You never know who might have a spare key from the previous tenant.

5. Custom Installations

Once you are satisfied with the property's condition, you can start making it your own. This is the time to hire a handyman for:

  • TV Mounting
  • Curtain and Blinds Installation
  • Furniture Assembly
  • Hanging pictures and mirrors

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Do I have to paint the apartment before moving out in Dubai?
Most tenancy contracts in Dubai require you to return the property in its original condition, which usually means repainting the walls to the original color, especially if you painted them a different color or left significant marks.
2. What is considered 'normal wear and tear'?
Normal wear and tear includes minor scuffs on walls, slight fading of paint, or small carpet indentations from furniture. It does not include large holes in the wall, broken tiles, or damaged appliances.
3. How can I ensure I get my full security deposit back?
To maximize your chances of getting your full deposit back, thoroughly clean the property, repair any damage beyond normal wear and tear, repaint if necessary, and ensure all appliances are in working order before the final inspection.
4. Should I hire a professional for move-out repairs?
Yes, hiring a professional handyman ensures repairs are done to a high standard, reducing the likelihood of the landlord deducting money from your deposit for subpar DIY fixes.
5. What should I check when moving into a new apartment?
Check the AC cooling, test all plumbing fixtures for leaks, ensure all electrical outlets work, inspect the walls and floors for existing damage, and document everything on a snagging list.
