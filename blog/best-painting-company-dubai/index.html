How to Choose the Best Painting Company in Dubai

Whether you are moving into a new villa in Dubai Hills, preparing to hand back your apartment in JLT to the landlord, or simply want to refresh your living space, a professional paint job can completely transform your home. However, with hundreds of painting contractors operating across the emirate, finding a reliable, high-quality service can be a daunting task.

Choosing the wrong company can lead to sloppy workmanship, hidden costs, and a stressful experience. At Handyman.ae, we have spent over 10 years perfecting our painting services, earning a 5.0-star rating from more than 262 satisfied customers. In this guide, we will walk you through exactly what to look for when choosing the best painting company in Dubai.

1. Verify Licensing and Insurance

The first and most crucial step is to ensure the painting company is fully licensed to operate in Dubai. A legitimate company will have a valid trade license issued by the Department of Economic Development (DED). Furthermore, they should carry comprehensive liability insurance. This protects you from being held financially responsible if a worker is injured on your property or if accidental damage occurs during the painting process.

2. Check Reviews and Reputation

In Dubai's competitive market, reputation is everything. Don't just rely on the testimonials displayed on a company's website. Check independent review platforms like Google Reviews to see what past clients have to say. Look for a company with a high overall rating (ideally 4.5 stars or above) and read through the comments to gauge their reliability, punctuality, and the quality of their finish.

For example, Handyman.ae is proud to be recognized as one of the best handyman services in Dubai, with a proven track record of excellence across all our home maintenance divisions.

3. Ask About the Quality of Paint Used

The quality of the paint used is just as important as the skill of the painter. Cheap paint will fade quickly, scuff easily, and may require multiple coats to achieve full coverage, ultimately costing you more in the long run. Ask the company what brands they use. Top-tier painting companies in Dubai typically use premium brands like Jotun, National Paints, or Benjamin Moore. They should also offer options for washable, anti-bacterial, or low-VOC (Volatile Organic Compounds) paints, which are safer for your family and pets.

4. Request a Detailed, Written Quote

Never accept a verbal estimate. A professional painting company will visit your property to assess the scope of work before providing a detailed, written quotation. This quote should clearly outline:

  • The total cost of labor and materials.
  • The specific brand and type of paint to be used.
  • The number of coats included (usually a primer and two topcoats).
  • Preparation work (e.g., filling cracks, sanding, taping edges).
  • The estimated timeline for completion.

Beware of quotes that seem unusually low; they often indicate hidden fees or the use of substandard materials.

5. Inquire About Preparation and Cleanup

A flawless paint job is 80% preparation. Ask the company about their prep process. Will they fill holes and cracks? Will they sand the walls smooth before painting? Additionally, clarify their cleanup policy. The best painting companies will cover your floors and furniture with drop cloths and ensure your home is left spotless once the job is done.

6. Understand Move-Out Painting Requirements

If you are a tenant preparing to vacate a property, you are likely required by your tenancy contract to return the apartment or villa to its original condition. This usually means painting the walls back to a standard white or off-white. Make sure the company you hire is experienced in "move-out painting" and understands the specific standards expected by Dubai landlords and property management companies.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to paint a 1-bedroom apartment in Dubai?
The cost to paint a standard 1-bedroom apartment in Dubai typically ranges to depending on the quality of paint used and whether the ceiling is included.
Do painting companies in Dubai provide the paint?
Yes, most professional painting companies, including Handyman.ae, provide high-quality paint (like Jotun or National Paints) as part of their service package. You can also choose to supply your own paint.
How long does it take to paint a villa in Dubai?
Painting a standard 3-bedroom villa usually takes between 3 to 5 days, depending on the scope of work (interior vs. exterior) and the number of painters assigned to the project.
Do I need to move my furniture before the painters arrive?
Professional painting companies will move heavy furniture to the center of the room and cover it with protective plastic sheets. However, you should remove small, fragile items and electronics beforehand.
Is move-out painting required for tenants in Dubai?
Yes, most tenancy contracts in Dubai require the tenant to return the property in its original condition, which usually means a fresh coat of white or off-white paint before moving out.
