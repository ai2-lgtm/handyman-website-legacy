Why Post-Ramadan Maintenance Is Essential in Dubai

Ramadan often coincides with rising temperatures and increasing humidity, which can exacerbate issues with air conditioning, kitchen appliances, and dust accumulation. The elevated indoor humidity from cooking and open windows during iftar can lead to mould growth and musty smells, especially in communities near the coast like JBR and Palm Jumeirah.

Ignoring these problems can affect indoor air quality and comfort as Dubai quickly transitions into the hot summer months.

Step 1: Air Conditioning and Ventilation Check

After Ramadan, it’s crucial to have your AC system inspected and cleaned. AC units working overtime during Ramadan evenings can accumulate dust and reduce efficiency.

Our technicians perform comprehensive AC duct cleaning and servicing to remove dust and improve airflow. Check out our detailed AC duct cleaning Dubai guide for more.

Step 2: Deep Clean Kitchens and Appliances

Increased cooking during Ramadan means grease and food residue can build up in your kitchen. Professional deep cleaning extends appliance life and prevents pest problems common in Dubai’s warm climate.

Step 3: Address Humidity and Mould

Humidity spikes after Ramadan, especially in coastal communities like Dubai Marina, can cause mould on walls, ceilings, and even inside wardrobes. A professional cleaning and mould remediation service will protect your home and health.

Step 4: Plumbing Inspection

Extra water use during Ramadan can stress your plumbing. Leaks or slow drains should be fixed early to avoid damage and ensure smooth daily routines.

Step 5: Electrical Safety Check

With extended use of kitchen equipment and lighting during Ramadan nights, it’s wise to have a licensed electrician check your circuits. This prevents tripping breakers or hazards.
