Warning Signs: Is Your Office Furniture a Disaster Waiting to Happen?

Look around your Dubai Internet City office right now.

Are you seeing any of these?

  • The Wobble Test: Gently push any desk or table. Does it sway more than 1-2 millimeters? This indicates loose connections or uneven legs.
  • Uneven Gaps: Examine cabinet doors and drawer fronts. Are the gaps around them inconsistent? Does one corner stick out more than another? This means the frame is misaligned.
  • Squeaks and Groans: Listen to your office chairs. Do they squeak or groan excessively with movement? Beyond normal wear, this often points to loose bolts or friction points from incorrect assembly.
  • Difficult Drawers: Do drawers stick, jam, or fall off their runners? This is a classic sign of a misaligned cabinet frame or improperly installed drawer slides.
  • Visible Gaps in Joints: Look at where two pieces of furniture meet, like a tabletop and its leg, or two sections of a modular desk. Are there visible gaps where they should be flush? This compromises stability.
  • Stripped Screw Heads: If you see screw heads that are rounded out or damaged, it means someone used the wrong tool or overtightened them. This weakens the connection significantly.
  • Missing Caps or Covers: Many flat-pack furniture pieces come with plastic caps to cover exposed screw heads or cam locks. If these are missing, it often means the assembly was rushed or incomplete.

Each of these signs is a red flag.

Each points to a problem festering beneath the surface.

Do not ignore them. Your furniture, and your business, depend on it.
