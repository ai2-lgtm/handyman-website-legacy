The Complete Checklist (Step By Step)

Step 1

Schedule all maintenance activities strictly after Iftar and before Suhoor to respect fasting hours and minimize tenant disruption; confirm access permissions with Al Barsha 1, 2, 3 residents and Mall of the Emirates commercial units; coordinate with Owners Associations to secure approvals and communicate schedules; ensure technicians are Ramadan-aware and equipped with appropriate PPE for evening work.

Step 2

Inspect kitchen plumbing systems for pre-Iftar load capacity; check all GROHE and Kohler faucets, grease traps, and drainage lines for blockages or leaks; clear kitchen floor drains and verify trap seals to prevent odor ingress; test water pressure and flow rates in compliance with Dubai Municipality plumbing codes; ensure all plumbing joints conform to Law 26/2007 standards.

Step 3

Perform thorough HVAC inspection focusing on FAHU and FCU units from Daikin, Carrier, or Trane; clean and replace air filters to maintain airflow during peak cooling; verify refrigerant levels and compressor operation to handle evening gatherings; inspect ductwork for leaks and corrosion due to salt humidity; calibrate thermostats for optimal cooling efficiency during post-Iftar hours.

Step 4

Test electrical safety devices including Schneider and ABB MCBs and RCDs to ensure circuit protection under increased Ramadan loads; check distribution boards for overheating or loose connections; verify emergency lighting and fire alarm systems comply with UAE Fire & Life Safety Code 2018; inspect backup power generators for readiness during power fluctuations; document all electrical tests for DEWA and RERA compliance.

Step 5

Clear soil stacks and vertical drainage pipes to prevent blockages caused by increased usage during Ramadan; use CCTV pipe inspection tools to detect cracks or deposits; flush soil stacks with high-pressure water jets to remove buildup; verify ventilation stacks are unobstructed to comply with Dubai Civil Defence regulations; apply corrosion inhibitors where necessary to mitigate salt air effects.

Step 6

Conduct water quality and hygiene checks in kitchens and washrooms, ensuring compliance with HACCP and DTCM standards; disinfect FAHU/FCU drip pans and condensate drains to prevent mold growth; check for Legionella compliance as per Dubai Health Authority (DHA) guidelines; verify grease trap cleaning schedules and grease disposal methods meet environmental regulations; train maintenance staff on Ramadan-specific hygiene protocols.

Step 7

Finalize maintenance by documenting all performed tasks with date-stamped photos and reports; update Ejari and RERA maintenance logs to reflect Ramadan-specific interventions; communicate completion status to Owners Associations and tenants; schedule follow-up inspections before the summer peak; advise clients on energy-saving tips and system usage during Ramadan evenings to prolong equipment life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can maintenance be performed during fasting hours in Ramadan?

Maintenance during fasting hours is generally discouraged in Dubai to respect residents’ observance of Ramadan. Handyman & Maintenance Services recommends scheduling all preventive maintenance post-Iftar and pre-Suhoor, in compliance with Dubai Municipality and RERA guidelines. This approach minimizes disruption and ensures technician safety and productivity while respecting cultural norms.

What brands are recommended for electrical safety checks in Al Barsha?

For electrical safety during Ramadan maintenance, certified brands such as Schneider, ABB, and Hager are recommended. These brands comply with DEWA’s electrical standards and Dubai Civil Defence requirements, ensuring reliable MCBs, RCDs, and distribution boards that protect against overloads and short circuits during peak Ramadan electricity consumption.

How does the Dubai climate affect Ramadan maintenance planning?

Dubai’s extreme heat, reaching up to 45°C, combined with Shamal winds carrying salt humidity, accelerates wear on HVAC and plumbing systems. Ramadan maintenance must therefore prioritize HVAC filter cleaning, refrigerant checks, and corrosion prevention in plumbing and soil stacks. Scheduling during cooler evening hours reduces heat stress on technicians and equipment, enhancing maintenance effectiveness.

Are there specific plumbing considerations for Ramadan in Al Barsha?

Yes, kitchen plumbing experiences increased load before Iftar due to meal preparation. Maintenance must include grease trap cleaning, drain line clearing, and pressure testing of faucets and pipes, ensuring compliance with Dubai Municipality Law 26/2007. Preventing blockages and leaks is critical to avoid interruptions during Ramadan meals and maintain hygiene standards set by HACCP and DTCM.

What HVAC systems are best suited for Ramadan peak cooling in Dubai?

HVAC systems from Daikin, Carrier, and Trane are preferred for their efficiency and durability in Dubai’s climate. During Ramadan, FAHU and FCU units must be serviced to handle peak cooling loads in evening hours, including filter changes, refrigerant top-ups, and duct inspections. Compliance with Empower and Emicool district cooling guidelines ensures optimal performance and energy efficiency.

How does skipping Ramadan maintenance affect insurance and resale?

Skipping Ramadan-specific maintenance can lead to insurance claim denials if damage results from neglect or non-compliance with Dubai Civil Defence and RERA maintenance obligations. Additionally, poor maintenance records negatively impact property resale value, as buyers and tenants prefer units with documented adherence to Dubai Municipality and Ejari standards, especially regarding HVAC and plumbing reliability during Ramadan.
