Common AC Repairs We Handle

❄️ AC Not Cooling

Diagnosis and repair for units blowing warm or weak air, including compressor, fan and airflow faults.

💧 Water Leaking

We trace and fix leaks from clogged drain lines, damaged trays or condensation before they damage ceilings or walls.

⚡ AC Tripping the Breaker

Electrical faults, overloads and wiring issues that keep tripping your breaker, diagnosed and repaired safely.

🔊 Noisy or Smelly AC

Rattling, buzzing or musty smells usually mean a loose part, dirty coil or mould — we find the cause and fix it.

🎛️ Thermostat & Remote Faults

Repair or replacement of faulty thermostats, remotes and control boards so your AC responds properly again.

🧪 Gas Top-Up & Leak Repair

Refrigerant leak detection, repair and gas top-up to restore full cooling performance.
