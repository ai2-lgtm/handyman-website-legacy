Seamless Apartment Move-In: Your Dubai Handyman Checklist

By Handyman.ae Team · Updated May 2026 · Expert Guide

Moving into a new apartment in Dubai, especially during the sweltering summer months when temperatures can exceed 45°C with 90%+ humidity, requires meticulous preparation beyond just unpacking boxes. The unique climate and building standards here mean certain checks are paramount to avoid immediate discomfort or costly repairs. From ensuring your AC is ready to combat the heat to verifying DEWA connections and understanding district cooling systems like Empower or Tabreed, a proactive approach saves significant hassle. This guide, from Dubai's handyman., outlines the crucial steps to take before settling in, ensuring your new home is truly move-in ready and comfortable from day one.

Pre-Move-In AC System Inspection and Maintenance

Before you even think about unpacking, a thorough inspection of your apartment's air conditioning system is non-negotiable in Dubai. The relentless heat demands peak performance. Our technicians typically check the outdoor condenser unit for cleanliness, ensuring fins are free from sand and debris that accumulate rapidly, especially in areas like JVC or Arabian Ranches. Inside, we inspect the evaporator coil for mould growth, a common issue with high humidity. We verify refrigerant levels and test the supply and return air temperatures; a healthy system should show a temperature drop of 8-11°C across the coil. Filters are cleaned or replaced, and drain lines are flushed to prevent blockages, which can lead to water leaks and ceiling damage. Neglecting this can result in poor cooling, higher DEWA bills, and even system breakdowns, leaving you in uncomfortable conditions.

Plumbing Integrity: Checking for Leaks and Water Heater Efficiency

A new apartment often hides plumbing surprises. Our first step is to check all visible pipes under sinks, behind toilets, and around washing machine connections for any signs of leaks or corrosion, particularly in coastal areas like Palm Jumeirah or JBR where salt air accelerates wear. We test all taps, showers, and flush mechanisms for proper function and water pressure. For water heaters, commonly Ariston or Ferroli brands, we verify the thermostat settings and check for any signs of leakage from the tank or connections. An inefficient water heater can consume excessive electricity, impacting your DEWA bill. It's also crucial to ensure all drainage points are clear and free-flowing to prevent backups, which can quickly become a significant issue in multi-story buildings.

Electrical System Verification and Safety Checks

Electrical safety and functionality are paramount. We begin by checking the distribution board (DB) for correctly labelled circuits and ensuring all circuit breakers are operational. We test all wall sockets for power and proper earthing, using a multimeter. Light fixtures are checked for functionality, and any flickering or non-responsive lights are investigated. It's common to find loose connections or outdated wiring in older properties, which can pose fire hazards. We also ensure that any installed appliances, such as ovens or cooktops, are correctly wired and earthed. A professional check helps identify potential overloads or faulty wiring that could lead to inconvenient power trips or, worse, electrical fires.

Appliance Installation and Functionality Testing

Many new apartments come with pre-installed appliances, or you'll be moving your own. We ensure all major appliances—washing machines (like Samsung or Bosch), dishwashers, refrigerators, and ovens—are correctly installed, levelled, and connected to their respective utilities. For washing machines, this includes checking water inlet and outlet hoses for leaks and ensuring proper drainage. Ovens and cooktops are tested for heating consistency and gas connections (if applicable) are checked for leaks. Incorrect appliance installation can lead to inefficiencies, damage to the appliance itself, or even water leaks and electrical faults. A professional setup ensures longevity and safe operation from day one.

Door and Window Security, Locks, and Seals

Security and insulation are key for comfort and energy efficiency. We inspect all external doors and windows for proper locking mechanisms. Common brands like Yale or Mul-T-Lock are checked for smooth operation and security. We also examine door and window seals for integrity. In Dubai's climate, compromised seals allow hot, humid air and sand to infiltrate, increasing AC load and leading to higher DEWA consumption. We adjust hinges and latches as needed to ensure a tight seal, preventing dust ingress and maintaining indoor air quality. This also contributes to sound insulation, a valuable asset in bustling areas like Downtown or Business Bay.

Addressing Sand Intrusion and Humidity Control

Dubai's desert environment means sand intrusion is a constant battle, especially in communities bordering open desert like Damac Hills or Arabian Ranches. We inspect window and door frames for gaps and recommend sealing solutions. Internally, we check for signs of sand accumulation in less obvious places, indicating potential entry points. Humidity control is equally vital; indoor humidity consistently above 60% can invite mould growth on cool surfaces within 48 hours. We assess ventilation, particularly in bathrooms and kitchens, and advise on strategies to maintain optimal indoor humidity levels, protecting your belongings and health from mould and mildew, which thrive in Dubai's humid conditions.

Understanding Your DEWA and District Cooling Connections

Navigating utility connections is a critical step. We can guide you through the process of ensuring your DEWA (Dubai Electricity and Water Authority) account is active and meters are read correctly. For apartments in areas like Business Bay or JLT, district cooling systems (Empower or Tabreed) are common. We help verify these connections are active and functioning, explaining how to manage your cooling consumption efficiently. Understanding your utility setup from the outset prevents unexpected bills or service interruptions. We also advise on checking water pressure and quality, ensuring your new home has reliable access to essential services without any hitches.

Essential Handyman Services for a Smooth Move

Beyond the initial checks, a smooth move often requires additional handyman support. This includes furniture assembly, especially for flat-pack items, ensuring they are sturdy and safe. We can assist with curtain installation, TV mounting, and hanging pictures, making your new apartment feel like home faster. For those with specific needs, babyproofing services are crucial for families with young children. Our team can also provide minor repairs or touch-ups, addressing any cosmetic issues before you fully settle in. These services ensure that all the small, yet important, details are handled efficiently, allowing you to focus on enjoying your new Dubai residence without the stress of DIY tasks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the most common issues found in new Dubai apartments?

The most frequent issues include inefficient AC systems due to sand ingress or lack of maintenance, minor plumbing leaks often exacerbated by hard water, and electrical sockets that may not be fully functional or properly earthed. Poor window and door seals leading to dust and heat intrusion are also very common, especially in older buildings or those near construction sites.

How can I prevent high DEWA bills in my new apartment?

To prevent high DEWA bills, ensure your AC system is serviced regularly, maintain optimal thermostat settings (24°C is often recommended), and use energy-efficient appliances. Checking for and sealing gaps around windows and doors is crucial to prevent heat and humidity from entering, reducing your AC's workload. Understanding your district cooling usage is also key.

Is it necessary to check for mould before moving in?

Absolutely. Dubai's high humidity, often exceeding 90% in summer, creates ideal conditions for mould growth, particularly on AC coils, bathroom ceilings, and behind furniture. A pre-move-in inspection can identify and address mould, preventing health issues and further spread. Early detection and remediation are vital for maintaining good indoor air quality.

What should I do if I find a major issue during my move-in inspection?

If a major issue like a significant water leak, non-functional AC, or electrical fault is discovered, document it immediately with photos and notify your landlord or property management. Under Ejari and RERA regulations, landlords are generally responsible for major maintenance. It's crucial to address these before signing any final acceptance documents.

Can handyman.ae help with setting up smart home devices?

Yes, our skilled technicians can assist with the installation and setup of various smart home devices, including smart thermostats, lighting systems, and security cameras. We ensure proper integration with your apartment's existing electrical and network infrastructure, helping you create a modern, efficient, and secure living space.

How often should AC filters be cleaned in Dubai?

Given Dubai's dusty environment and high AC usage, it's recommended to clean or replace AC filters every 1-3 months. Regular cleaning significantly improves air quality, maintains AC efficiency, and extends the lifespan of the unit. Neglecting filters can lead to reduced cooling performance and increased energy consumption.
